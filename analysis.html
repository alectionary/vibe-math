Reading the classifications

Precisely stated means expressed as mathematical definitions, equations or theorems. Conventionally proved means the dissertation supplies or cites a mathematical argument. Machine checked requires a proof-assistant artifact; ordinary computer algebra, exhaustive search, numerical solutions and empirical comparisons do not establish this status. An assumption is not a failed theorem, and a conditional theorem is a valid theorem with restricted scope.
