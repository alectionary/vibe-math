## What the recovered texts establish

F02 Theorems 3.2(a) and 3.3(a) state weak existence for fractional-diffusion exponent alpha >= 1, but weak uniqueness only for alpha >= 5/4. Their assumptions include a smooth calming map, global Lipschitz continuity, boundedness and a derivative bound; the spatial setting is periodic. Consequently, these theorems do not prove the dissertation's alpha=1 calmed velocity–vorticity weak-uniqueness target. The comparison is now based on the actual theorem statements rather than an abstract-level lead. This is a statement-scope comparison, not an independent verification of the proofs. [Full public text, manuscript pp. 6 and 11–12](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/395935877_Velocity-vorticity_formulation_of_the_three_dimensional_generalized_Navier-Stokes_equations_Effect_of_algebraic_calming_on_well-posedness_convergence_determining_wavenumber_and_regularity_criteria).

P28 Section 8 proposes extensions to general block arrays, algebraic quasi-n-adic families, distance bounds for n >= 3, comparisons with quasi-cyclic bounds, and further control of dimension from signature support. These are broad tasks without fixed proposed numerical bounds; they are not silently promoted into fully specified conjectures. Its quantum-distance program overlaps the direction already inventoried from P11. P11's later affine-support rank theorem and particular full-converse conjecture are relevant progress, not a complete solution of P28's general dimension program. [Public manuscript, Section 8](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/387080373_Codes_based_on_dyadic_matrices_and_their_generalizations).

The recovered P20 questions are historical material from a withdrawn source. The current arXiv notice cites problems with low-diameter cases. Recovering an earlier PDF resolves an access gap, not the mathematical defects. [Current withdrawal notice](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.00110) · [Historical v3 manuscript](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2209.00110v3).

## Provenance and formalization annotations

The source manifest preserves the earlier unsuccessful/abstract-only observations. P28 and F02 hashes identify the recovered local full-text transcriptions, not the publisher PDF bytes. Those transcriptions were assembled from the publicly readable web page; local line n corresponds to web source line L(n-1). All article body text and references of these public manuscript copies were recovered, while a few non-article link/blank lines outside the body are marked omitted. These local texts and source PDFs remain ignored by Git.

Publisher PDF metadata endpoints returned empty bodies, and older publisher PDF links returned 404. The normal publisher PDF control displayed an access restriction for P28. Open-access indexes label P28 and F02 open, but that metadata did not supply a working publisher download. Public full-text pages, rather than index labels, supplied the texts used here. No purchase or message to authors was made.

The ten new entries comprise five broad P28 directions, one F02 background formulation request and four historical P20 entries. This is not a global absence claim, and no new Lean formalization was produced.
