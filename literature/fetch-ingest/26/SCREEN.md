## What the paper establishes

Runqing Yang, Hongwang Li, Lina Fu, and Yongxin Liu. Advance access 29 August 2013; *Briefings in Bioinformatics* 15(5), 814–822. https://doi.org/10.1093/bib/bbt061.

The paper defines a conditional phenotype of each trait given the others, estimates it by shrinkage, and then runs separate LASSO coordinate-descent association scans. The authors say the procedure matches a staged structural-equation solution and, in their simulations, matches joint mapping in estimation and detection power. The yeast eQTL analysis is an application.
