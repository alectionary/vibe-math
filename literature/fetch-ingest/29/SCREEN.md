## What the paper establishes

Anna A. Igolkina, Georgy Meshcheryakov, Maria V. Gretsova, Sergey V. Nuzhdin, and Maria G. Samsonova. *BMC Genomics* 21(Suppl 8):490 (2020). https://doi.org/10.1186/s12864-020-06833-2.

The paper defines mtmlSEM, an extension of a LISREL model with SNP columns that are restricted to a single nonzero entry, and separates single-trait and pleiotropic effects. Parameters are estimated by maximum likelihood after polychoric or polyserial correlations, with Gibbs sampling when the parameter count is large. On 404 chickpea accessions and 16 traits the authors report about 230 associated SNPs, about 60 of them pleiotropic, and prediction accuracy comparable to the breeding-value figures they cite.
