## Results proved or derived in the dissertation

- Theorems 16, 26, 28: A strict dominating-pair graph must satisfy the stated diameter/asteroidal-number alternative; the converse is explicitly not asserted. Further theorems constrain how asteroidal sets meet dominating paths. (PDF pp. [29](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1273&context=computerscidiss#page=29), [35](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1273&context=computerscidiss#page=35), [37](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1273&context=computerscidiss#page=37)).

- Theorem 29: Chordal HDP graphs are characterized by the specified absence of asteroidal triples with 3-spread, giving the stated recognition-time bound. (PDF pp. [38](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1273&context=computerscidiss#page=38), [39](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1273&context=computerscidiss#page=39)).

- Theorems 33, 56, 57: Additional results give a diameter bound under partition conditions on an asteroidal set, and show that a graph with dominating-target number three and a golden triple has a polar target of size three. (PDF pp. [43](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1273&context=computerscidiss#page=43), [44](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1273&context=computerscidiss#page=44), [65](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1273&context=computerscidiss#page=65), [66](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1273&context=computerscidiss#page=66)).

## Scope and interpretation notes

- A recognition theorem for chordal HDP graphs does not establish polynomial recognition for all HDP graphs. The exponent stated in the dissertation depends on its cited algorithmic ingredients. (PDF pp. [39](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1273&context=computerscidiss#page=39), [71](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1273&context=computerscidiss#page=71)).
