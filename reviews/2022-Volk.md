# Extremal Problems in Graph Saturation and Covering
Adam Volk · 2022 · Mathematics

[Source record](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/mathstudent/112/) · [Original PDF](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1119&context=mathstudent)

Investigates extremal subgraph counts in saturated or covered graphs, characterizes tree-saturating spiders, and maximizes independent sets for selected covering constraints.

## Results proved or derived in the dissertation

- Theorems 3–5 and 9: The thesis proves an asymptotic cherry-count result for clique-saturated graphs, exact optimization over a candidate family for stars in star-saturated graphs, and existence bounds for multipartite saturated constructions. (PDF pp. [13](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1119&context=mathstudent#page=13), [14](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1119&context=mathstudent#page=14), [23](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1119&context=mathstudent#page=23), [24](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1119&context=mathstudent#page=24), [32](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1119&context=mathstudent#page=32), [33](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1119&context=mathstudent#page=33), [36](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1119&context=mathstudent#page=36), [43](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1119&context=mathstudent#page=43), [44](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1119&context=mathstudent#page=44)).

- Theorem 15; Proposition 25: A spider is tree-saturating exactly in the classified types. Necessary conditions for more general tree-saturating trees are not the same as a full classification. (PDF pp. [78](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1119&context=mathstudent#page=78), [79](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1119&context=mathstudent#page=79), [80](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1119&context=mathstudent#page=80), [81](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1119&context=mathstudent#page=81), [82](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1119&context=mathstudent#page=82), [83](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1119&context=mathstudent#page=83), [84](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1119&context=mathstudent#page=84), [85](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1119&context=mathstudent#page=85)).

- Theorems 6–7: For path-covered and matching-covered graphs, fixed-size independent-set counts are bounded by specified extremal candidate families. (PDF pp. [104](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1119&context=mathstudent#page=104), [105](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1119&context=mathstudent#page=105), [106](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1119&context=mathstudent#page=106), [107](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1119&context=mathstudent#page=107), [108](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1119&context=mathstudent#page=108), [109](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1119&context=mathstudent#page=109)).
