# Operator Theoretic Methods for Coupled PDE in General Geometries
Yuhao Mu · 2026 · Mathematics

[Source record](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/dissunl/519/) · [Original PDF](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1586&context=dissunl)

Develops pressure elimination and operator representations for coupled fluid–elasticity PDEs on bounded Lipschitz geometries, with analytical well-posedness and finite-element applications.

## Hypotheses and modeling assumptions

- Bounded Lipschitz geometry does not remove the domain/interface hypotheses, constitutive parameters, function-space conditions, or prescribed boundary conditions. Local nonlinear strong solutions require data in the generator domain. FEM bounds require the specified meshes, spaces, and stability conditions. (PDF pp. [16](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1586&context=dissunl#page=16), [17](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1586&context=dissunl#page=17), [18](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1586&context=dissunl#page=18), [36](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1586&context=dissunl#page=36), [104](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1586&context=dissunl#page=104), [129](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1586&context=dissunl#page=129)).

## Questions, conjectures and remaining work

- The introduction presents global well-posedness of the full untruncated nonlinear system in general geometry as a larger unresolved goal, including the 2D and small-data 3D directions. (PDF pp. [14](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1586&context=dissunl#page=14), [15](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1586&context=dissunl#page=15)).

## Scope and interpretation notes

- Global well-posedness for a truncated equation and local well-posedness for the full equation are different claims. Neither establishes unrestricted global Navier–Stokes/fluid–structure regularity. (PDF pp. [13](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1586&context=dissunl#page=13), [14](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1586&context=dissunl#page=14), [15](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1586&context=dissunl#page=15), [104](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1586&context=dissunl#page=104)).
