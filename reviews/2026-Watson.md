# Cohomological Support Varieties Along Ring Maps
Ryan Watson · 2026 · Mathematics

[Source record](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/dissunl/446/) · [Original PDF](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1513&context=dissunl)

Studies the behavior of cohomological support varieties under restriction and extension of scalars along local ring maps, and develops a computational construction.

## Results proved or derived in the dissertation

- Theorems 1.0.1 and 3.3.9: Support varieties of suitable modules transform by inverse image under the map induced by a local homomorphism. The proof factors the map and uses the weakly regular and surjective cases. (PDF pp. [10](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1513&context=dissunl#page=10), [50](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1513&context=dissunl#page=50), [51](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1513&context=dissunl#page=51), [52](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1513&context=dissunl#page=52), [53](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1513&context=dissunl#page=53), [54](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1513&context=dissunl#page=54), [55](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1513&context=dissunl#page=55)).

- Theorem 3.4.4 and corollaries: A local map of finite flat dimension gives the stated dimension inequality for supports after derived extension of scalars; complete-intersection descent/localization consequences follow. (PDF pp. [57](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1513&context=dissunl#page=57), [58](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1513&context=dissunl#page=58), [59](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1513&context=dissunl#page=59), [60](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1513&context=dissunl#page=60), [61](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1513&context=dissunl#page=61), [62](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1513&context=dissunl#page=62)).

- Chapters 4–5: Chapter 4 develops the resolution/construction needed to compute supports, extending Eisenbud–Shamash methods; Chapter 5 recasts results in derived-category language. (PDF pp. [11](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1513&context=dissunl#page=11), [63](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1513&context=dissunl#page=63), [64](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1513&context=dissunl#page=64), [65](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1513&context=dissunl#page=65), [66](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1513&context=dissunl#page=66), [93](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1513&context=dissunl#page=93), [94](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1513&context=dissunl#page=94)).

## Hypotheses and modeling assumptions

- Rings are commutative Noetherian and local in the principal comparison results. The affine-variety theorem assumes algebraically closed residue fields and finite generation over both rings. The dimension inequality additionally assumes finite flat dimension; the weakly regular base-change theorem uses its own flatness hypotheses. (PDF pp. [10](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1513&context=dissunl#page=10), [34](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1513&context=dissunl#page=34), [37](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1513&context=dissunl#page=37), [38](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1513&context=dissunl#page=38), [54](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1513&context=dissunl#page=54), [60](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1513&context=dissunl#page=60)).
