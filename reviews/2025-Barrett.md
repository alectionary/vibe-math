# A Zariski-Nagata Theorem for Smooth Toric Surfaces
Jordan Vincent Barrett · 2025 · Mathematics

[Source record](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/dissunl/294/) · [Original PDF](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1293&context=dissunl)

Seeks a toric analogue of Zariski–Nagata: describing symbolic powers by vanishing-order conditions in Cox rings, with criteria for smooth toric surfaces.

## Results proved or derived in the dissertation

- Theorem 5.2.5: The Generic Point Criterion reduces the toric symbolic-power equality for all subvarieties of a smooth toric surface to equality of ordinary and symbolic powers of its generic-point ideal. (PDF pp. [51](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1293&context=dissunl#page=51), [52](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1293&context=dissunl#page=52)).

- Theorem 6.0.2; Corollary 6.0.7: The Quadrant Criterion supplies sufficient smoothness conditions; a corollary establishes Toric Zariski–Nagata for smooth complete toric surfaces with at most five rays. (PDF pp. [54](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1293&context=dissunl#page=54), [55](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1293&context=dissunl#page=55), [56](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1293&context=dissunl#page=56), [57](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1293&context=dissunl#page=57), [58](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1293&context=dissunl#page=58), [59](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1293&context=dissunl#page=59)).

- Theorem 6.0.10: Normality of the relevant generic-point quotient is a sufficient condition for the symbolic-power equality in the stated smooth complete surface setting. (PDF pp. [61](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1293&context=dissunl#page=61)).

## Questions, conjectures and remaining work

- The closing discussion leaves universality across toric surfaces unresolved, and suggests further normality criteria, higher-dimensional analogues, and behavior under star subdivision. (PDF pp. [64](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1293&context=dissunl#page=64)).

## Scope and interpretation notes

- The title is broader than the strongest unconditional family proved: at-most-five-ray complete surfaces and other surfaces meeting sufficient criteria. Do not infer a theorem for every smooth toric surface. (PDF pp. [57](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1293&context=dissunl#page=57), [61](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1293&context=dissunl#page=61), [64](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1293&context=dissunl#page=64)).
