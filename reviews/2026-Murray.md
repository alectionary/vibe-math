# Bass Numbers of Veronese Submodules and Lifting the Frobenius Trace
Taylor Jeffrey Murray · 2026 · Mathematics

[Source record](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/dissunl/449/) · [Original PDF](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1516&context=dissunl)

Two algebraic projects: transfer of Bass-number finiteness through Veronese functors, and a characteristic-two obstruction to lifting Frobenius trace on symmetric determinantal rings.

## Mathematical setup and definitions

## Results proved or derived in the dissertation

- Theorems 10–12; Corollary 10: Under the graded-generation hypotheses, finite Bass numbers pass from M over R to the Veronese module over the Veronese subring. The thesis gives formulas away from the homogeneous maximal ideal and applications to local cohomology. (PDF pp. [66](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1516&context=dissunl#page=66), [67](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1516&context=dissunl#page=67), [69](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1516&context=dissunl#page=69), [70](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1516&context=dissunl#page=70), [71](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1516&context=dissunl#page=71), [72](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1516&context=dissunl#page=72), [73](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1516&context=dissunl#page=73), [74](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1516&context=dissunl#page=74)).

- Theorem 8: A graded Matlis-duality statement relates graded Betti numbers to Bass numbers, furnishing a tool for the finiteness argument. (PDF pp. [57](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1516&context=dissunl#page=57), [58](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1516&context=dissunl#page=58)).

- Theorems 16, 18, 19: For symmetric determinantal rings in the characteristic-two setting, the Frobenius trace does not lift to the indicated differential operator over the integral ring. The proof uses a nonzero local-cohomology obstruction and explicit differential operators. (PDF pp. [101](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1516&context=dissunl#page=101), [102](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1516&context=dissunl#page=102), [106](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1516&context=dissunl#page=106), [133](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1516&context=dissunl#page=133), [134](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1516&context=dissunl#page=134), [135](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1516&context=dissunl#page=135)).

## Hypotheses and modeling assumptions

- The Veronese results require a positively graded finitely generated algebra with generator degrees coprime to the chosen positive integer, and a graded module with finite Bass numbers. The trace project is in characteristic two, with generic symmetric matrices of size at least three. (PDF pp. [3](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1516&context=dissunl#page=3), [66](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1516&context=dissunl#page=66), [67](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1516&context=dissunl#page=67), [101](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1516&context=dissunl#page=101)).

## Scope and interpretation notes

- Theorem 16 uses p in its statement while the immediately preceding text specifies p = 2. The review preserves that characteristic-two scope; an all-primes reading would overstate the contribution. (PDF pp. [100](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1516&context=dissunl#page=100), [101](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1516&context=dissunl#page=101)).

- The Bass-number formula excludes the homogeneous maximal ideal; finiteness is proved there without the same displayed formula. (PDF pp. [67](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1516&context=dissunl#page=67), [74](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1516&context=dissunl#page=74)).
