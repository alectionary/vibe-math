EXACT BOUNDED QUESTION

Let L>=2, R=F2[x]/(x^L-1), and E:Fin3 x Fin4 -> ZMod L. All 12 base entries
are monomials x^E(i,c); a zero exponent is an identity circulant. Normalize
the first row and column to zero. Use the source's symmetric ring LP
construction, with star equal to transpose followed by x -> x^(-1):
  C  = expand(B),
  HX = expand([B tensor I4 | I3 tensor B*]),
  HZ = expand([I4 tensor B | B* tensor I3]).
The ring tensor product precedes expansion. Quantum length is 25L.

Assume:
  (1) the classical Tanner graph of C has girth at least eight;
  (2) neither full source row nor column RCPC holds;
  (3) for each choice of middle row k, with other rows i,j, no value occurs
      on three or more columns among E(i,c)+E(j,c)-2E(k,c);
  (4) the transpose classical distance dT is at least dC;
  (5) the CSS logical quotient has positive dimension.

dC is the minimum nonzero binary kernel weight of C. Define dT analogously
for C transpose, with infinity if its kernel is zero. Define dX over
ker(HZ) minus rowspan(HX), dZ with X/Z exchanged, and dQ=min(dX,dZ).
For the usual Pauli distance, supply the min-sector/union-of-support bridge.
All row spans are complete binary row spans, not just individual generators.

Answer contract:
Source distance-preservation question -> a universal sufficient lower bound
under explicit strengthened tests -> faithful written and Lean proofs of
the source-matrix, both-sector and full-span statements, OR an independently
verified qualifying base and a nontrivial logical of weight < min(dC,12).
A counterexample needs a certified classical lower bound strictly above the
witness weight; computing the exact quantum distance is unnecessary.
