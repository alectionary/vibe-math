Source-to-proof audit for L-F25-distance-preservation

Source: Raveendran–Declercq–Vasic, arXiv2503.07567v1 (10 March2025).
Relevant formal model: equation(9), fully populated monomial3x4 B over the
cyclic binary group algebra, with B1=B2=B. Definitions2–5 are RCPC.
The source's SectionVI asks for sufficient constraints yielding dQ=dC.
The refuted implication in this artifact is our proposed restriction of that
question; the source does not state that implication as a conjecture.

Counting/coordinate convention
  i in Fin3, j in Fin4, fiber s in ZModL.
  Classical variable (j,s); check (i,t); incidence t=s+Eij.
  Left quantum coordinate ((a,b),s), a,b in Fin4.
  Right quantum coordinate ((a,b),s), a,b in Fin3.
  Quantum checkX ((i,j),t); checkZ ((j,i),t).
  Fixed family integer labels [[0,0,0,0],[0,1,5,3],[0,2,10,7]].
  Monomial zero exponent is an identity block. There are no absent base blocks.
  Star means transpose plus exponent negation. Binary transpose alone is not
  silently substituted for the ring star before expansion.

Source object -> formal object -> bridge/evidence

1. Expanded classical B
  SourceDistance.C uses SourceModel.P(e,t,s)=if t=s+e then1 else0.
  SourceDistance.c_action proves multiplication by that matrix equals the
  coefficient boundary in CyclicCover. This is the actual3L by4L matrix.
  FamilyBaseGraph.incidence_bridge transports finite base incidence atL11
  through explicit finProdFinEquiv maps. FamilyBaseDistance.kernel_row and
  packWord_weight transport the source kernel and Hamming weight to the
  finite BitVec certificate. RREF reconstruction covers EVERY source-kernel
  vector, not only the listed basis vectors or tested codewords.

2. HX=[B tensor I4 | I3 tensor B*]
  SourceModel.HX gives its individual coefficients with the coordinate order
  above. SourceModel.x_span_left and x_span_right prove arbitrary binary
  row combinations equal FamilyWords.stabilizerLeft/stabilizerRight.
  SourceModel.inSpanX_iff quantifies over EVERY lifted-check coefficient
  function and identifies the full row span with the abstract image.
  This is not a single-generator test or a bounded stabilizer search.

3. HZ=[I4 tensor B | B* tensor I3]
  SourceModel.HZ gives its individual coefficients.
  SourceModel.z_action proves the full check action equals FamilyWords.Syndrome.
  SourceModel.z_span_left/z_span_right and inSpanZ_iff cover the other full
  row span. FamilyWords.css_commutation proves HZ HX^T=0 in coefficient form.

4. Classical and quantum distance
  DistanceFamily.ClassicalHasWeight minimizes nonzero classical kernel words.
  DistanceFamily.LogicalX is ker(HZ) outside the full HX image.
  DistanceFamily.QuantumHasWeight includes both LogicalX(u,v) and
  LogicalX(transpose(u),transpose(v)). SourceDistance.logicalX_iff and
  logicalZ_iff prove those are exactly the two source CSS sectors. Both
  square qubit blocks transpose; the cyclic fiber does not change sign.
  SourceDistance.classical_weight_iff/quantum_weight_iff identify the actual
  source predicates with the predicates used in the Nat.find minima.
  Explicit classical and quantum witnesses prove the minimized sets nonempty.
  The quantum witness's nonspan proof itself guarantees a nonzero logical
  quotient, so no vacuous zero-dimension distance convention is being used.

5. The logical witness
  FamilyRing: fixed E identity U B^T=bar(B)^T V over a general characteristic-
  two commutative ring with an integer-indexed multiplicative monomial map.
  FamilyWords.witness_kernel: independently checks all12 coefficient check
  types with arbitrary fiber t and arbitrary L.
  FamilyWords.augmentation_stabilizer: all binary coefficients w.
  FamilyWords.augmentation_witness and witness_nontrivial: separating value1.
  FamilyWords.witness_weight_le: support containment in an11-element list;
  overlaps and characteristic-two cancellation can only lower the weight.
  No minimal-distance search result is used as this theorem's premise.

6. Classical cover lower bound
  CoverWeight.push is binary fiber parity, not ordinary occupied-support image.
  CyclicCover.boundary_push and kernel_push prove the parity-check bridge.
  CyclicCover.projected_support_stopping retains occupied fibers even when
  the parity projection vanishes. Partner variables have distinct base-column
  types; that is why projection does not merge the two neighbors of a check.
  CoverWeight.zero_push_weight_doubles treats cancellation separately.
  FamilyClassical11.actual_classical_lower certifies the full base kernel;
  FamilyBaseGraph.code_lower_twelve includes kernel and Hamming-weight bridges.
  FamilyBaseGraph.stopping_lower_six includes the actual incidence bridge.
  DistanceFamily.classical_lower_twelve splits zero/nonzero parity projection
  and proves the lower bound for every positive L with11 dividing L.
  L may be even. L=0 is excluded by NeZeroL, and11|L then forces L>=11.

7. Girth
  VoltageCycles uses the actual classical incidence t=s+Eij.
  The no-four/no-six voltage hypotheses concern distinct base row/column types.
  A simple alternating4-cycle must have distinct row and column types; a
  simple alternating6-cycle in this monomial cover must use3 distinct types
  on each side. Its arithmetic closure is therefore one of the forbidden
  voltages. No non-simple closed walk is counted as a simple cycle.
  FamilyNoShort.no_four/no_six prove those voltage conditions for every11|L
  by mapping a hypothetical equality through ZMod.castHom to the checked
  base11 equality. Bipartiteness and simplicity make the absence of4/6 the
  usual girth>=8 assertion.

8. RCPC
  For3 rows there is one distinct pair and one self-pair. RowPairing quantifies
  i,j,k distinct with E_i+E_j=2E_k in every column (Definition5).
  For4 columns there are two disjoint pairs. ColumnPairing quantifies four
  distinct indices with equal paired sums in every row (Definition2).
  A forbidden pairing atL would map to one modulo11. No converse of the
  source RCPC theorem is assumed.

9. Connectedness
  FamilyConnected.Step is the actual undirected classical Tanner incidence,
  with separate variable/check vertices. Reach is its reflexive transitive
  closure, so connected states that every pair is joined by a finite walk.
  The normalized zero row/column connect all types at one fiber; exponent1
  permits a fiber increment; natural representatives reach every ZModL fiber.
  This excludes explaining the family as disjoint copies of the L11 example.

10. Transpose-code comparison
  FamilyTranspose.boundary is sum_i x(i,t+Eij), with a PLUS exponent because
  this is C^T. DistanceFamily.transpose_c_action proves that actual matrix
  action explicitly. The polynomial Bezout identity in FamilyTranspose.third_period
  forces period1 of the third component. TransposeConstant proves all three
  components constant and their binary sum zero. TransposeDistance proves
  every nonzero such word has weight2L and exhibits one.
  DistanceFamily.classical_distance_upper uses an actual classical word with
  two complete variable fibers, of weight2L. Stronger_counterfamily therefore
  shows the mixed logical drop survives the additional condition that no
  nonzero transpose word is lighter than dC.

11. Standalone finite certificate
  Counterexample11 uses the original L11 labels [[0,0,0,0],[0,8,7,2],[0,5,3,1]].
  Source11 and QuantumRows11 define actual source-expanded BitVec rows;
  CSS11 proves every X/Z check pair commutes. The separator annihilates the
  full rowSum image. ClassicalDistance11 reconstructs all source-kernel words.
  Counterexample11.diagnostic_refuted gives dC=12 and dQ<=11. This certificate
  is retained independently of the family assembly.

12. General parameter template
  ParametricRing.mixed_cycle proves the polynomial identity for arbitrary
  integer parameters a,b and any characteristic-two commutative ring with
  the stated monomial multiplication law. ParametricWords uses parameters
  directly in ZModL and proves the coefficient identity, augmentation value
  and11-term support bound, including coincident/cancelling parameter cases.
  GeneralLP.HX/HZ/C are the same source Kronecker coefficients with arbitrary
  E. GeneralLP.classical_action and z_action give the actual matrix actions;
  x_span_left/right quantify arbitrary lifted-check coefficients. Its LogicalX
  predicate uses the actual kernel and the entire actual row span.
  ParametricSource.source_parametric_obstruction includes actual matrix CSS
  commutation, nontriviality and physical-coordinate Hamming weight.
  GeneralLP.nonzero_classical_exists proves the classical minimum nonempty.

13. Congruence family
  ParametricProjection.projected_exponents checks every source exponent under
  an additive homomorphism p with p(a)=1,p(b)=5. The hypotheses are arithmetic,
  not lower-distance assumptions. Classical_lower applies the checked base
  kernel and the structural stopping proof through CoverDistance.lower_bound.
  Actual_classical_lower converts this to the generic source C matrix.
  No_four/no_six and pairing exclusions map hypothetical equalities to the
  forbidden base11 equalities. Cyclic_congruence_counterfamily specializes to
  11|L and reduction of(a,b) to(1,5). No oddness, unit-parameter, unchanged-rank
  or connectedness premise is silently added.

14. Fixed/general model equivalence
  ParametricSpecialization.exponent_matrix, HX_matrix, HZ_matrix,
  classical_matrix and left_word identify the parameter(1,5) with the original
  fixed definitions. In particular, HX/HZ use Int.cast_neg to reconcile
  negation before an exponent cast with negation in ZModL. LogicalX proves
  equivalence of the complete physical kernel/span predicates, not just of
  the displayed witness.

15. Classical upper word and alternative cover route
  ClassicalUpper20.kernel directly checks the20-term polynomial word in all
  three source classical equations for arbitrary positive L. Its nonzero
  proof uses reduction modulo11 to separate the0,2,3,6 positions in one
  component. Its support-list proof permits cancellations and yields at most20.
  Classical_distance_upper uses the existing source minimum definition.
  CoverDistance.lower_bound is the general min(d_base,2*s_base) argument;
  FamilyCoverAlternative assembles the family lower12 using the generic
  TannerSix theorem and actual source incidence. The finite stopping-set
  search certificate remains separately checked.

16. Sector symmetry
  SourceSymmetry.swapQ transposes each square qubit block and fixes the fiber.
  Transpose_weight proves the actual support-cardinality equality through
  that permutation. Sector_weights identifies the source X/Z logical weight
  spectra for the fixed family. For arbitrary exploratory E, the written
  identity HX((i,j),t;q)=HZ((j,i),t;swapQ(q)) gives the same row/qubit
  permutation argument. This generic application is outside the compiled
  SourceSymmetry scope and does not turn C++ searches into Lean lower bounds.

Formal status is determined by successful source-bound records in

Independent finite evidence
  independent_family.py constructs U,V supports directly, without importing
  research.py. independent_verify.py builds each binary coefficient matrix
  from explicit circulant powers and Kronecker products, checks CSS, syndrome,
  augmented stabilizer rank, complete classical/transpose enumerators and
  actual Tanner BFS. These computations support L11/L22 instances, not the
  universal quantifier. They supplement the Lean proof rather than define it.

Current-resolution/priority limits

  absence claim. Classical stopping/cover ingredients credited to Kelley etal.
  Newer full-rank upper-bound and lift-versus-base theorems are compared with

Search implementation correction
  The original C++ two-place completion shortcut omitted singles. No admitted
  Lean declaration depends on a negative C++ result. All earlier negative
  search claims were withdrawn and replayed after repair; search-repair.json
  identifies superseded and corrected records, independent regression tests
  and unchanged original input hashes. External exclusions remain outside
  the admitted Lean closure even after successful replay.

Universal-parameter boundary audit
  independent-degenerate.json verifies all285 pairs atL1..9 in dense matrices,
  including a=0, b=0, nonunits and cancelling support terms. The helper now
  takes polynomial support modulo2. The earlier three L22 saved supports
  are bytewise the same coordinate lists under the corrected helper.
  These are finite tests of the universal Lean theorem, not its premises.

Independent SAT attempt
  sat_crosscheck.py encodes the full dense HZ kernel and an OR over a basis
  of ker(HX)/rowspan(HZ), which detects nonmembership in the entire HX span.
  Small full-word oracles and the known upper witness pass; the L13 lower
  search returns UNKNOWN at900 seconds. No SAT UNSAT or Lean lower certificate
  is claimed. The quotient dimension, solver pin and exact hashes are saved.

Separate generic structural closure
  LowWeightChecks.kernel_check_zero_or_two covers arbitrary binary Tanner
  kernels under explicit degree/no4/no6 premises. Its stopping/support and
  parity bridges are checked. PermutationFixedColumns.two_fixed_columns_impossible
  covers explicit source matching equations, identity row map and two fixed
  columns. Both have separate source-bound and isolated authored-dependency
  builds. Neither theorem silently asserts the missing full weight7 source
  support-to-pattern classification. The finite144-pattern integer identities
  are independently rechecked, but their universal catalog/source Lean bridge
  remains a proposed follow-up. See STRUCTURAL-ROUTE.txt.

Final semantic handoff checks
  The source Kronecker products occur before expansion: length25L, not25L^2.
  The main result requires positiveL with11|L; fixed-family connectedness and
  transpose characterization have their separately recorded broader scopes.
  The universal parameter obstruction includes cancellations and even covers.
  The proposed next truncated target keeps normalized first row/column and
  the frozen strengthened entry tests explicit. Its choice is adaptive after
  the diagnostic; no general condition is marked proved. SourceSectionV-B
  already claims the stabilizer-weight exclusion, so weight7 work is a source
