We prove the quantum minimum-distance bound dQ >= min(dC,12) for a class of symmetric cyclic lifted-product quantum low-density parity-check (QLDPC) codes. The result addresses a bounded follow-up to Raveendran, Declercq and Vasic's question about sufficient conditions for quantum-classical distance equality, posed in On the Minimum Distances of Finite-Length Lifted Product Quantum LDPC Codes (2025), Section VI, DOI:10.48550/arXiv.2503.07567 (https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2503.07567).

For normalized, fully populated 3x4 monomial bases over F2[x]/(x^L-1), we assume classical Tanner girth at least eight, exclusion of full column partitions, exclusion of three equal column values of each row form E(i,c)+E(j,c)-2E(k,c) for distinct rows, and transpose-code distance at least dC. The proof classifies all mixed kernel supports of weight at most eleven into three block-count profiles and resolves them using 183,312 checked integer certificates. The argument accommodates composite cyclic lift lengths without division. Lean verifies the complete source-to-distance implication for the expanded binary matrices, both full stabilizer spans, both CSS sectors and ordinary Pauli union weight. The broader sufficient-condition question for dQ=dC remains open.

Keywords: quantum error correction; quantum LDPC codes; lifted-product codes; quasi-cyclic codes; CSS stabilizer codes; quantum minimum distance; row and column partition constraints; Tanner graph girth; normal graphs; integer certificates; Lean 4; formal verification.
