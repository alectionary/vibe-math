The entry tests and truncated bound here are our proposed follow-up.

For L>=2, let R=F2[x]/(x^L-1), B(i,j)=x^E(i,j), with all12 entries present,
i inFin3 and j inFin4. Zero exponent means an identity circulant. Normalize
E(0,j)=E(i,0)=0. Star transposes and sends x to x^(-1). Form in R, then expand:
  C  = expand(B),
  HX = expand([B tensor I4 | I3 tensor B*]),
  HZ = expand([I4 tensor B | B* tensor I3]).
There are25L qubits. U(j,k,s) is the left block; V(l,i,s) is the right block.
The HZ equation at check(j,i,t) is
  sum_k U(j,k,t-E(i,k)) + sum_l V(l,i,t+E(l,j)) = 0.

Assume classical Tanner girth>=8; no full source row/column RCPC; no value
appearing on three columns among E(i,c)+E(j,c)-2E(k,c), for each middle rowk;
dTranspose>=dC; and positive CSS logical dimension. Both distances are binary
kernel minima, with dTranspose=infinity for a zero transpose kernel.
A pure X logical lies in ker(HZ) outside the ENTIRE row span of HX. Swap X/Z
for the other sector. The usual Pauli weight is union of the two supports.

2. ACTUAL INCIDENCE, ZERO-OR-TWO CHECKS AND PARITY

A left coordinate(j,k,s) meets check(j,i,s+E(i,k)) for each i: degree3.
A right coordinate(l,i,s) meets check(j,i,s-E(l,j)) for each j: degree4.
Two same-sector qubits sharing two checks would give a classical four-cycle;
a mixed pair has only one possible check type. Three same-sector qubits in
a quantum six-cycle give a classical six-cycle. In a mixed six-cycle, the
single opposite-sector qubit would use the same check type twice, forcing two
of the supposedly distinct checks equal. Thus classical no4/no6 implies no4/
no6 for the actual HZ incidence. ActualIncidence.lean proves these statements
and the exact nonzero-entry correspondence, for arbitrary E and positive L.

Apply LowWeightChecks.kernel_check_zero_or_two, a checked check-rooted Tanner
tree argument, to any source kernel word of weight<=11. Every neighboring check
meets its support in exactly two coordinates. Source incidence has just been
bridged; a statement about an unrelated abstract graph is not substituted.

Let a_j be the number of selected left coordinates with first indexj and b_i
the number of selected right coordinates with second indexi. Summing the HZ
equations over t gives a_j+b_i=0 modulo2, for every j,i. All seven counts have
one common parity. BlockParity.lean proves this directly in the source model;
BlockCounts.lean relates binary masses to actual support cardinalities.

3. NORMAL GRAPH AND THE THREE COUNT PROFILES

Join the two selected coordinates at each occupied check. The resulting normal
graph is simple: two checks joining the same pair would be a quantum four-cycle.
It is triangle-free: the three edge checks must be distinct (otherwise one
check would meet three selected coordinates), and distinct checks would give a
quantum six-cycle. Its left/right vertex degrees are3/4, respectively.
NormalGraph.lean proves the generic triangle-free implication and the standard
Mantel/Turan edge bound, on the actual selected subtype rather than all qubits.

Within bucket(j,i), let m_ji count U-U edges and c_ji count U-V edges. The socket
identity is a_j=2m_ji+c_ji, and c_ji<=b_i. Consequently
  sum_i max(a_j-b_i,0)/2 <= floor(a_j^2/4).
The analogous V-V inequality is
  sum_j max(b_i-a_j,0)/2 <= floor(b_i^2/4).
Same-parity counts make each nonzero difference even. The upper bounds are
Mantel's triangle-free edge bound applied to each induced same-group graph.
Distinct check types count distinct normal edges by four-cycle exclusion.

For mixed supports of total weight<=11, sort the four a counts and three b
counts separately. There are exactly44 common-parity positive-total profiles.
The displayed inequalities leave precisely:
  weight7:  a=(1,1,1,1), b=(1,1,1);
  weight10: a=(2,2,2,0), b=(2,2,0);
  weight11: a=(5,1,1,1), b=(1,1,1).
CountPatterns.lean proves completeness of the bounded descending-list enumerator
and checks the44-case arithmetic implication in the Lean kernel. A separate
exhaustive socket-matching program independently finds these same three profiles,
with1,64 and120 labeled matchings, respectively. SocketEdges, SocketArithmetic,
BucketCounts, BucketSums and LocalMantel now prove the displayed inequalities
from the actual expanded source kernel. SourceProfiles.lean proves the complete
source-to-sorted-profile theorem, including sorting completeness and parity.

Pure U supports split into classical C-kernel words, one per first-index group;
pure V supports split into C-transpose kernel words. A nonzero pure support of
weight<dC is therefore impossible under dTranspose>=dC.

4. NORMALIZATION AND THE CONSTANT LABEL CASE

Row/column permutations relabel both tensor coordinates and check indices.
For row/column gauge e'(i,j)=e(i,j)+r_i+c_j, the transformed words are
  U'(j,k,s)=U(j,k,s+c_j+c_k),
  V'(l,i,s)=V(l,i,s-r_l-r_i),
and transformed stabilizer coefficients are w'(i,j,s)=w(i,j,s-r_i+c_j).
GaugeTransport.lean checks the syndrome and both entire span formulas. These
maps are coordinate permutations, with inverse shifts. Normalizing after a
row/column relabeling therefore preserves word weights and full quotients.
The partial-row values change by one column-independent constant; equality
on any three columns is preserved. Normalization.lean checks these form identities,
the normalized full tests and no-four/no-six transport. GaugeWeight.lean checks
actual support weight; SpanCoefficients.lean checks equivalence of the complete
source stabilizer spans under gauge shifts. For clarity, unrestricted gauge shifts need not preserve the literal zero
form of an RCPC equation. Here we always re-normalize after a row/column
permutation. If F_i=E(i,a)+E(i,b)-E(i,c)-E(i,d), re-normalizing with first rowr
changes it to F_i-F_r. Since the old first row has F_0=0, all new F_i vanish
if and only if all old F_i vanish. Likewise a row-pair form
G_c=E(i,c)+E(j,c)-2E(k,c) changes to G_c-G_h when columnh becomes first;
the old first column has G_0=0. Thus full normalized RCPC is preserved, and
threefold equality of partial-row values is preserved. Tanner girth is
preserved by the corresponding check/variable coordinate permutations. We may put the missing weight10 row/column at0 and the
exceptional weight11 first-index group at3.

For singleton U groups j and singleton V groups i, let k_j be the U second
index, l_i the V first index, and s_j,t_i their fibers. Cross matching says
  s_j+E(i,k_j)=t_i-E(l_i,j).
If two k_j agree, subtracting their equations forces the column difference
E(l_i,j)-E(l_i,j') independent of i. Four-cycle exclusion then forces all l_i
equal. Conversely, a repeated l_i forces all k_j equal. Thus the maps are either
both constant or both injective. SingletonLabels.lean proves this algebraic
alternative. SupportSingletons.lean extracts actual singleton coordinates and
matching equations from the source kernel and applies the alternative. In the injective case l is a permutation of
three rows; k is a permutation (weight7) or an injective triple (weight11).

In the constant case, write k_j=K and l_i=H. The equations give a common z with
  s_j=z-E(H,j),  t_i=z+E(i,K).
These are exactly the corresponding coordinates of HX row((H,K),z).
At weight7 the word is that stabilizer. At weight11, add that complete
stabilizer: all three singleton U coordinates and all three V coordinates
cancel, leaving a pure U kernel support of size at most6. If the original
weight11 were below dC, the remainder must be zero. The original word is then
a stabilizer. No restricted set of generators is substituted for the full span.

6. THE SIX WEIGHT10 EXCEPTIONS ARE CLASSICAL ALTERNATIVES

Each of these cases implies 5E(1,c)=5E(2,c)=0 for some c!=0; normalized row0
supplies the remaining equation. Put K={s in ZMod L: 5s=0}. Its size is at most5.
The binary word equal to1 on {0,c} x K and0 elsewhere is nonzero. At each
classical check, its two contributions agree: the exponent in column0 is zero,
and the exponent in columnc lies in K, so translating K preserves its indicator.
The two equal binary contributions cancel. Its weight is2|K|<=10.
TorsionClassical.lean proves this for actual source C and, more generally, the
weight<=2n construction for an n-torsion subgroup.

Hence a source word of weight10 in one of these patterns cannot be strictly
lighter than dC. This is an alternative in the proof, not a counterexample.
The independently checked L10 boundary example in boundary-L10.json has dC6,
dTranspose10, a nontrivial quantum word of weight10, and the displayed torsion
word of weight10. These upper witnesses do not assert exact quantum distance.

7. CONCLUSION OF THE WRITTEN REDUCTION

Suppose a nontrivial X logical has weight w<min(dC,12). It is nonzero, w<=11,
and cannot be pure-sector. Source incidence gives the normal graph, common
parity and local edge bounds, so its profile is one of7,10,11. Constant label
cases are stabilizers or reduce to a forbidden small classical word. Injective
cases belong to the complete finite catalogs. Every catalog case contradicts
a hypothesis, except the six classical alternatives, which imply dC<=10=w.
This contradicts w<dC. Thus no such X logical exists.

GeneralSymmetry.lean proves actual X/Z kernel, full-span and weight transport
for arbitrary E, not just the previous fixed family. PauliBridge.lean proves
that an X-sector lower bound transfers to the Z sector and bounds the union
support of any nontrivial Pauli pair. Therefore the written reduction gives
the intended dQ>=min(dC,12), with exactly the stated hypotheses.

9. POST-LEMMA PRIMARY COMPARISONS

(28June2023), SectionIV.6/Statement12, prove a lower bound with a divisor equal
to the support-intersection subgroup size. Their two-element group-algebra
construction and that bound do not establish this 3x4 matrix-base criterion.

Gideon Lee, Bicycle Race: small two-block codes on the Pareto frontier,
arXiv:2610.13880v2 (12October2026), describes exact code-specific distance
engines, equivalence reductions and rank-based search targeting. These are
relevant computational background, rather than a general theorem implying
the target here. Its additional questions and four unresolved fixed-code
distances have been harvested, with exact block-identification gaps held.

Okada and Kasai, Rate-2/3 Girth-8 (3,18)-Regular Quantum LDPC Codes from
Two-Branch Finite-Field Bases and CPM Lifts, arXiv:2606.27130v2
(5September2026), Sections3.4/3.7, use pairing at checks, colored triangle-free
normal graphs and exact integer consistency certificates. These related
methods are prior work and are credited. Their v2 proves exact distance18
for one34542-qubit code and a weight18 affine-shift ceiling for a different,
fixed finite-field base, for prime lift degree>19. The v1-only upper-bound
wording is superseded by that v2 result. Neither theorem supplies our universal
3x4 symmetric-LP statement. Our all-cyclic-modulus integer identities do not
divide by a coefficient that might vanish at a composite or small modulus.
