Question and provenance

The motivating source is Raveendran–Declercq–Vasic, arXiv2503.07567v1,
equation9, Definitions2–5 and SectionVI. Its broader design question asks for
conditions giving dQ=dC. The strengthened 3x4 entry tests and the truncated
min(dC,12) target are a project-proposed follow-up, RQ-F25-truncated-distance-12.
Neither a completed truncated bound nor its supporting lemmas are counted as a
new canonical completion of the broader equality question.

Objects

For positive L, e : Fin3 -> Fin4 -> ZMod L specifies every monomial entry x^e
of the fully populated base B over F2[x]/(x^L-1). A zero exponent denotes an
identity circulant, not a missing block. GeneralLP.C is the actual 3L-by-4L
binary expansion. GeneralLP.HX and GeneralLP.HZ are the actual 12L-by-25L
coefficient matrices of [B tensor I4 | I3 tensor B*] and
[I4 tensor B | B* tensor I3]. No support-only surrogate replaces these matrices.
The formal statements use NeZero L; the requested L>=2 supplies this instance.
No oddness, primality or field assumption is imposed on the cyclic modulus.

Hypothesis correspondence

- Classical girth>=8: TannerSix.NoFour (CEdge e) and NoSix (CEdge e).
  The incidence is the nonzero-entry relation of actual C. ActualIncidence
  transfers these properties to the quantum check incidence and proves degrees.
- First row/column zero: explicit row_zero and column_zero fields; zero means
  the exponent identity. Exactly six normalized exponent variables remain.
- Full column RCPC: NoColumnPartition ranges over all four distinct column
  labels. For every partition into two pairs it excludes equality of their
  exponent-vector sums. This is Definition2 at n=4, checked against the primary
  text in the second round as well as the original source screen.
- Full row RCPC: Definition5 at m=3 is rowForm e i j k c=0 for all columns,
  where rowForm=e(i,c)+e(j,c)-2e(k,c). NoTripleRow is stronger: it forbids equal
  rowForm values on any three distinct columns. CriterionScope proves that this
  already excludes full-row RCPC; the redundant separate premise is unnecessary.
- Transpose comparison: sourceTransposeDistance is the infimum of actual nonzero
  binary C-transpose kernel weights. DistanceMinima proves it is infinity for a
  zero transpose kernel, and converts dTranspose>=dC into TransposeAtLeast e dC.
- Classical minimum: sourceClassicalDistance is Nat.find over actual nonzero
  C-kernel word weights. GeneralLP.nonzero_classical_exists ensures existence;
  DistanceMinima proves both attainment and its universal lower-bound property.

Logical quotient and weight

fullSpanX and fullSpanZ quantify every binary linear combination of every HX/HZ
row. The X word must be in ker HZ and the Z word in ker HX. A Pauli pair is
nontrivial exactly when its two components are not both in those full spans.
Its weight is the cardinality of the UNION of supports, not their sum.
GeneralSymmetry checks sector transport for arbitrary e, and PauliBridge checks
the union-weight implication. DistanceMinima defines the actual quantum minimum
as an extended-natural infimum of these Pauli weights and proves the lower-bound
equivalence. A nontrivial Pauli witness makes it finite; with no such witness it
is infinity. Thus the lower bound also covers that boundary case, while the
source's positive-CSS-dimension regime has the usual finite Pauli distance.
No positive-dimension conclusion is inferred merely from compiling a bound.

Coverage chain

SourceProfiles starts with the actual source kernel and proves that every mixed
support of weight<=11 has precisely one of the sorted count profiles at weights
7,10,11. Pure sectors are handled by actual C and C-transpose lower bounds.
SourceSeven handles the complete seven-singleton profile. TenEncoding constructs
actual finite patterns from canonical weight-ten supports. SourceEleven derives
the actual weight-eleven K2,3 graph, proper row coloring, missing-row permutation,
two orientations and cross matches, and includes the complete constant-row
stabilizer cancellation branch. ProfileTransport preserves every entry/girth
and distance hypothesis, the actual kernel, full spans, weight and group counts
under the required permutations and re-normalization. CountCanonical supplies
the marker choices, so canonical placement is not an extra input assumption.

Closed checks prove full integer identities of the source matching matrix,
including all fiber variables. KernelCatalog interprets them in additive groups;
TargetObstructions and PatternRealizations apply them to actual source incidences.
CoverageCore assembles the checks into quantified coverage using a balanced
interval proof. Coverage10 and Coverage11 must be admitted before final use.
The six torsion-five alternatives give an actual classical word of weight<=10;
they contradict a proposed weight-ten word strictly below dC. They are not
qualifying counterexamples, and division by5 is never used.

TwelveConditional is explicitly conditional on the independent weight-ten finite
coverage. ThroughEleven must discharge that premise with complete_coverage10.
DistanceTwelve then states the literal source minimum bound. A completed result
requires these exact sources, all imported research sources and their matching
kernel records; generated data, discovery output and Python replay are separate.

Limits

The conclusion is a sufficient LOWER bound min(dC,12). It is not an exact quantum
distance, a necessary criterion, a quantum upper bound or the unrestricted dQ=dC
answer. The known weaker-criterion counterfamily and all prior frozen experiments
remain unchanged. The literature ledger credits related methods and records
bounded prior-art coverage, including the unavailable native-X service. No new
paper/question cohort is created by this formalization continuation.
