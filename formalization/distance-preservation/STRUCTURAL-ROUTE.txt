COMPLETED GENERIC PROOFS

LowWeightChecks.lean proves the following general implication for a binary
Tanner incidence E. Assume every variable has at least three distinct check
neighbors and there are no simple four- or six-cycles. Every binary kernel
word with support size at most eleven meets every check in zero or two
selected positions. All variables and checks in the theorem are arbitrary
finite/decidable types as required by its signature; no source LP hypothesis
is substituted for a missing graph proof.

The check-rooted counting proof is a classical Tanner-tree argument. If one
check has four selected neighbors, choose two other checks at each of those
four variables, and a selected partner at each new check. Four-cycle exclusion
keeps the eight partners outside the four roots and separates partners of one
root; six-cycle exclusion separates partners of different roots. There are
at least twelve selected variables. Below twelve, each check degree is at
most three; binary parity leaves only zero and two.

This proof and its parity/kernel-support bridge pass the normal source-bound
build and a separate rebuild against freshly rebuilt authored dependencies.
Only propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound occur in the printed closures.
See auxiliary-verification.json. This is separate from the frozen50-module
counterfamily root.

An independent exact finite example shows the generic threshold is sharp:
a17-by12 ordinary binary matrix has every variable degree three, girth eight,
kernel dimension one and minimum distance twelve. Its nonzero word meets one
check in four positions. The construction is four root variables at one check,
eight private partners and an eight-cycle connecting the partners, with the
partners of each root opposite on the cycle. low-weight-sharpness.json records
the whole matrix, exact enumeration and BFS check. This sharpness example is
external finite evidence, not a Lean certificate or an LP quantum instance.

PermutationFixedColumns.lean separately proves that two nonzero cyclic
elements killed by two must coincide. It then excludes the following explicit
source matching equations when k has two distinct fixed columns p,q:

  s_j + E(i,k(j)) = t_i - E(i,j), for every i inFin3 and j inFin4,

under classical four-cycle exclusion. The equations force all differences
(E(i,p)-E(i,q))-(E(0,p)-E(0,q)) into cyclic2-torsion. Four-cycle exclusion
makes the three row differences distinct, contradicting the size of that
torsion set. auxiliary-fixed-columns-verification.json binds the normal and
isolated checks. The theorem's interface is the displayed matching equations;
it is not a complete logical-support classification.

THE RESTRICTED144-PATTERN DIAGNOSTIC

Consider exactly one left coordinate U(j,k(j),s_j) for each j inFin4 and
one right coordinate V(l(i),i,t_i) for each i inFin3, with k and l permutations.
Assume their check incidences pair across all twelve (j,i) check types.
The source syndrome matching equations are

  s_j+E(i,k(j))=t_i-E(l(i),j).

Thus F(i,j)=E(i,k(j))+E(l(i),j) is row/column separable. For normalized E,
its six consistency equations are
F(i,j)-F(i,0)-F(0,j)+F(0,0)=0, i=1,2, j=1,2,3.
These are integer linear equations A*v=0 in the six nontrivial exponents.

The frozen diagnostic enumerates all24*6=144 permutations and searches only
integer combinations of these six equations with coefficients in[-2,2].
It finds125 forced short-cycle identities,12 full-RCPC identities, one cyclic
two-torsion collision, and leaves six maps unclassified under that fixed search.
The latter six are single column transpositions with identity row map; they
have two fixed columns. The identity-map torsion case also has fixed columns.
The separate checked fixed-column obstruction applies to those seven cases
under the stated matching equations. The original diagnostic counts remain
unchanged.

verify_pattern_certificates.py independently reconstructs A and every target
form by evaluating scalar basis exponent matrices. It checks all saved integer
combinations with Python integers and checks coverage of all144 maps. The
catalog is permutation-patterns.json; its independent check is
pattern-certificate-verification.json. No coefficient cap was retuned.

At the level of a written finite-certificate argument, the specified
permutation-pattern family is therefore forced into RCPC or a forbidden cycle.
The universal source-equation/catalog Lean bridge is not supplied here.
No inference that every weight-seven logical has this form is part of this
finite diagnostic.

SOURCE-CLAIM VERIFICATION ROUTE —NOT A COMPLETED LEAN RESULT

One supporting verification task is the following claimed source consequence:

  For a normalized fully populated source3x4 cyclic base, classical girth>=8 and a nontrivial
  weight-seven logical imply a row or column RCPC pairing.

Normalized means that the first row and first column of E are zero. Keep this
hypothesis explicit: literal RCPC equalities can change under arbitrary row
gauge shifts. The finite catalog uses exactly this normalized model.

RDV2025 Theorem2 explicitly states the forward RCPC implication. SectionV-B
and the conclusion assert the recipe's stronger exclusion of stabilizer-weight
logicals. Accordingly, the weight-seven work is an audit/formalization of a
source claim, not a new open mathematical question. P34's conclusion asks how partition
constraints translate to Tanner-graph properties. Reuse those canonical source
identifiers; this task adds no new author-posed question or eligible target.

A proposed proof route has the following bridges still to complete and audit.

1. Instantiate the generic zero-or-two theorem on the actual source quantum
   Tanner incidence. The prior P34 girth characterization and the new source
   matrices use the same circulant/star convention; their coordinate transport
   and degree-three/four statements must be explicit.

2. Turn a weight-seven kernel support into a simple graph joining its paired
   variables through checks. No four-cycles gives unique edges and no six-cycles
   gives triangle-freeness. The source left/right degrees are three/four. Parity
   makes the number of selected left variables even, so a selected right variable
   exists. Its four neighbors are independent. Each has at least three neighbors
   among the three remaining vertices, forcing the proposed K(4,3) geometry.
   This argument must be checked with the actual matrix incidence.

3. Show that the complete cross-block geometry contains each first left index
   and each second right index once, producing maps k,l and fibers s,t. If one
   map repeats, source four-cycle exclusion should force both maps constant.
   In that case the matching equations identify exactly one translated HX row,
   which is a stabilizer. Otherwise both maps are permutations.

4. Transport the144 integer certificates to the source exponent equations,
   apply the fixed-column obstruction, and cover the other CSS sector with the
   actual row/qubit permutation and full span. The restricted catalog does not
   replace these steps or the stabilizer/nontriviality argument.

These are proposed bridges, with the generic lemmas and exact catalog already
available. The source claim is not yet fully checked in this artifact. The broader
distance-equality condition remains open even if this weight-seven case succeeds:
the proved counterfamily has a weight-eleven logical.

PREFERRED NEXT FOLLOW-UP: A TRUNCATED DISTANCE-PRESERVATION CONDITION

Our proposed bounded mathematical target is:

  dQ >= min(dC,12)

for normalized fully populated3x4 cyclic bases satisfying all of:
  classical girth at least8;
  no full row/column RCPC;
  no value repeated three times among E(i,c)+E(j,c)-2E(k,c) over the four
    columns, for each choice of middle row k and other rows i,j;
  transpose classical distance at least dC.

The truncated target is an adaptive recommendation made after the diagnostic.
Its exclusion range matches the corrected frozen104-base campaign: no logical
of weight<=min(dC-1,11). The inputs and bounds remain unchanged; this does not
turn that diagnostic into a successful proof of the original full-equality
condition. The finite sample supports no
universal conclusion. The new generic zero-or-two lemma applies throughout
this range and is sharp at12, giving a natural bounded classification problem.
The source-claimed weight7 case still needs its faithful verification; the
new mathematical bottleneck is controlling other mixed supports, especially
weights8..11. Pure-block/classical comparisons and the smallest-weight cases
must also be bridged, with both sectors and full spans. For the usual Pauli
distance, include the union-of-support/min-sector bridge: a nontrivial Pauli
has a nontrivial pure component whose weight is no larger.

This target is a restricted sufficient lower-bound question within the original
source design problem. It does not promise equality for classical distance
above12, or silently assume a general quantum upper bridge. It is our proposed
follow-up, not an author-posed numbered conjecture or a new canonical target.

WHY THIS TARGET IS PREFERRED

The truncated problem uses one finite range of support geometries and the
checked generic lemmas, while aiming for a universal entrywise design result.
The144-pattern work supports the already-claimed weight7 case. The alternative
finiteL13 quantum14 certificate has an independent upper witness and a corrected
external exclusion through13, but no Lean lower certificate; its separate SAT
attempt returned UNKNOWN at900seconds. Keep that as a deferred finite option.

No discovery-time or formalization-time estimate is inferred. Completion of
generic helper lemmas is not completion of either source question. Canonical
completion delta for this research block remains zero.
