Distance loss in a girth-eight, RCPC-free lifted-product family
Research block: 24–25 October 2026 (Pacific research date: October 24)
Canonical source target: L-F25-distance-preservation

RESULT

The proposed criterion “classical girth at least eight and no row/column
partition constraint imply d_Q=d_C” is false. This was our diagnostic
hypothesis, not a conjecture attributed to the source authors.

A fixed fully populated monomial 3-by-4 base gives a counterfamily at every
positive lift size divisible by 11:

                 [0  0   0  0]
       E =       [0  1   5  3],             L=11q, q>=1.
                 [0  2  10  7]

The entries are exponents modulo L. Zero means an identity circulant.
The classical Tanner graph is connected and has girth exactly eight. Neither
source RCPC pairing exists. Nevertheless,

                   d_Q <= 11 < 12 <= d_C <= 20.

These are general Lean proofs with source-matrix and full stabilizer-span
bridges. The transpose classical code has distance 2L, so a lighter transpose
code does not explain the mixed logical. Both quantum CSS sectors are included
in the definition of d_Q. The result needs an upper bound on d_Q; its exact
value for the whole family is not established here.

There is also a general obstruction template:

       E(a,b) = [[0,0,0,0],[0,a,b,3a],[0,2a,2b,b+2a]].

For every positive cyclic L and every a,b in ZMod L, this template has a
nontrivial source logical of weight at most 11. If 11 divides L and a,b reduce
to 1,5 modulo 11, respectively, the classical lower bound 12, the absence of
four/six cycles and the absence of RCPC hold as well. Thus the template gives
many further counterexamples. Connectedness, transpose distance 2L and the
classical upper bound 20 above refer to the fixed specialization a=1,b=5.

Raveendran, Declercq and Vasić, On the Minimum Distances of Finite-Length

including the self-pair in the odd case.
https://arxiv.org/html/2503.07567v1#S6

Earlier project results about girth, stopping sets and displayed low-weight

stabilizer quotient. The P34 girth work supplies the entrywise cycle vocabulary;
the stopping-set work supplies the structural lower-bound ingredient used in
the cover proof; earlier CSS distance work supplies the full-quotient standard
for checking a logical. This investigation supplies a distance obstruction
that passes the earlier local tests. It is a new target within a familiar
construction, with quantum distance as the invariant being investigated.

The source's Theorem2 associates RCPC with the m+n stabilizer-weight scale
(seven for a3x4 base). The partial-pairing construction here leaves a
weight-eleven nontrivial logical. That is already enough to lose distance
when the classical minimum is at least twelve.

The source's broader sufficient-condition problem remains open in this project.
This result refutes our explicit proposed criterion, including its variant
requiring transpose distance at least d_C. It does not refute the possibility
of useful sufficient conditions or the source's RCPC implication. Canonical
source-target completion delta: zero.

MATRIX CONVENTIONS

Let R=F_2[x,x^(-1)]/(x^L-1), B_ij=x^(E_ij), and bar mean x->x^(-1).
The source star is B*=bar(B)^T. Binary expansion uses
P^e(t,s)=1 iff t=s+e modulo L. The matrices are

  C  = expand(B),                         dimensions 3L by 4L,
  HX = expand([B tensor I4 | I3 tensor B*]),
  HZ = expand([I4 tensor B | B* tensor I3]), dimensions 12L by 25L.

The tensor products occur in the ring before binary expansion. There is one
cyclic fiber, so the quantum length is 25L.

Left and right qubit blocks are U in R^(4 by 4) and V in R^(3 by 3).
The displayed (U,V) is a pure X logical supported in both product qubit blocks.
Consequently its weight also upper-bounds the usual Pauli logical distance
directly. No general quantum lower bound is part of the counterfamily claim.
The HZ cycle equation is U B^T + bar(B)^T V=0. In coefficients:

  sum_k u(j,k,t-E(i,k)) + sum_l v(l,i,t+E(l,j)) = 0.

An arbitrary HX stabilizer, with binary coefficients w(i,j,s), has blocks

  u(i,j,s)=sum_k w(k,j,s+E(k,i)),
  v(i,j,s)=sum_k w(i,k,s-E(j,k)).

Nontriviality is tested against every row combination. SourceModel/SourceSectors
prove the matrix actions and both full spans; SourceWordBridge proves equivalence
with minima over all source binary vectors. Transposing both square blocks
exchanges X/Z sectors and preserves weight; the fiber is not negated. The
compiled SourceSymmetry theorem covers the fixed family. For exploratory
bases, the same written row/qubit-permutation argument is used; their lower
search bounds are outside the Lean closure.
GeneralLP proves the same matrix identities for arbitrary monomial 3-by-4 E.
ParametricSpecialization proves that this general model specializes to the
original fixed model, including the star's cast/negation identities.

THE PERSISTENT LOGICAL

For the fixed specialization, use

       [1    0            0       0   ]
  U =  [0    x^-2         0       0   ],
       [0    0            x^-10   0   ]
       [x^-2 x^-4+x^-2    x^-7    x^-4]

       [0 0 1]
  V =  [0 1 0].
       [1 0 0]

Direct multiplication gives U B^T=bar(B)^T V. There are at most 11 nonzero
binary coordinates: eight terms in U and three in V. The identity holds
before imposing a cyclic quotient, so it survives every lift size, including
even sizes.

The linear functional

  ell(u)=sum_s u(0,0,s)+sum_s u(1,0,s) in F_2

vanishes on every HX stabilizer: translation permutes each fiber sum, and the
two resulting copies of sum_(k,s) w(k,0,s) cancel. But ell(U)=1. The witness
therefore lies outside the entire stabilizer span.

For general a,b, replace the first three diagonal entries by
1,x^(-2a),x^(-2b), and the last row by
[x^(-2a),x^(-4a)+x^(-2a),x^(-(b+2a)),x^(-4a)]. Keep V unchanged.
ParametricRing proves the polynomial identity; ParametricSource proves the
actual matrix-kernel, full-span nonmembership, weight bound and CSS commutation
for every positive L and every parameter pair. Coincident terms can cancel,
which is why the universal statement uses weight at most 11.

The row-pairing relation holds on the first three columns. For a=1,b=5 it
fails on the fourth by one unit. Correcting that column produces the mixed
operator above. Excluding full RCPC therefore does not exclude this mechanism.

CLASSICAL LOWER BOUND THROUGH A COVER

At L=11 the fixed expanded base has 44 variables, 33 checks, rank 31 and kernel
dimension 13. Complete enumeration gives minimum nonzero weight 12. Lean
verifies source-row reconstruction of every kernel word and checks the complete
8192-case weight certificate. Explicit incidence, dot-product and Hamming-weight
bridges connect the finite certificate to the actual coefficient functions.

Reduce the fiber modulo 11. For a nonzero classical codeword z upstairs, let y
be its binary parity projection, and T the image of its occupied support,
retaining fibers whose parity cancels. Projection commutes with the check map.

If y is nonzero, wt(z)>=wt(y)>=12. If y=0, each occupied fiber contains at
least two selected coordinates. T is a nonempty stopping set: a selected
neighbor of an upstairs check has another selected neighbor in a different
base-column type, and projection preserves that distinction. The base stopping
lower bound is six. Hence wt(z)>=2|T|>=12.

CoverDistance proves the reusable lower bound min(d_base,2*s_base). The argument
uses no odd-cover or unchanged-dimension premise. A finite stopping certificate
and an independent structural proof both establish the required base bound.
TannerSix proves that variable degree at least three and no four/six cycles
force a nonempty stopping set to have at least six vertices. FamilyTreeStopping
and FamilyCoverAlternative apply that argument to this source family.

The classical ingredients are credited to Kelley, Sridhara, Xu and Rosenthal,
Pseudocodeword Weights and Stopping Sets, ISIT 2004, Theorem III.1 and the
following note. Projected cover-codeword support being stopping and the
classical girth/tree bound are established predecessors.
https://user.math.uzh.ch/kelley/isit2004_Chicago.pdf

ParametricProjection uses any additive projection p:ZMod L -> ZMod 11 with
p(a)=1,p(b)=5. It maps E(a,b) to the certified base. Reduction modulo 11 supplies
such a projection under the stated cyclic congruences.

GIRTH, RCPC, CONNECTEDNESS AND TRANSPOSE

All distinct row/column choices have nonzero four/six-cycle voltages modulo 11.
A zero voltage upstairs would project to a forbidden equality. VoltageCycles
proves the bridge from those conditions to simple cycles in the actual Tanner
incidence t=s+Eij.

For three rows, RCPC requires distinct i,j,k with E_i+E_j=2E_k in every column.
For four columns it requires a partition into two disjoint pairs with equal
column sums. Both fail in the certified base, and any pairing upstairs would
project to one downstairs. These are source Definitions 5 and 2, respectively.

For the fixed specialization, an explicit simple eight-cycle has check vertices
(0,0),(1,0),(2,1),(1,1) and variable vertices
(0,0),(1,-1),(0,1),(1,0), in alternating order. FamilyGirth checks its edges and
distinctness. The normalized zero row/column connect all types at one fiber;
entry one at (row1,column1) gives a four-edge walk incrementing the fiber.
FamilyConnected proves connectedness for every positive L.

For the fixed base, a polynomial Bezout identity in the four transpose equations
forces period one in the third component. It then forces all three components
constant, with binary sum zero. Every nonzero transpose codeword has weight 2L,
and one exists. TransposeDistance and the actual C^T action bridge prove this
for all positive L.

There is also an explicit classical word f with components

  x^4+x^5+x^7+x^8+x^9+x^11,
  x^2+x^3+x^6+x^7+x^8+x^11,
  1+x^2+x^3+x^6,
  1+x^4+x^5+x^9.

It satisfies B f=0 before quotienting. For 11|L it remains nonzero and has
weight at most 20. ClassicalUpper20 proves the resulting d_C upper bound.
The bounded polynomial-multiplier search that found it is retained separately;
no exact large-L classical minimum follows from that search.
