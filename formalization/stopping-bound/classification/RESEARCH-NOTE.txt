PROOF AND LEAN TRANSLATION

1. Generic classifier — ClassificationSearch.lean, classifiesList_sound.
The prior degree-one extension lemma says that if S lies in a stopping support T
and a check has degree one in S, some neighbor of that check lies in T\S.
The new classifier branches over EVERY such neighbor. At fuel0 it accepts if a
check of degree one remains (so S cannot equal a stopping T) or if S has a
specified good form. At positive fuel, encountering no degree-one check rejects
rather than silently assuming a minimality result. All other children must
accept. The proof uses induction, S.Nodup, S subset T and |T|<=|S|+fuel.
At fuel0 the cardinality bound forces S=T. At positive fuel the stopping
extension lemma provides a child inside T; the all-children Boolean certificate
supplies the induction premise. Thus a true certificate proves the requested
property for every stopping completion in the exact stated size range.

2. Good leaves — ClassificationData.lean; branch certificates — ClassificationBranches.lean; two-root combination — ClassificationKernel.lean.
