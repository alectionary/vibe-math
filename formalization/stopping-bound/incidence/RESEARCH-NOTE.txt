RESULT AND SCOPE

For every positive integer t, put ell=63t and work over F_2[x]/(x^ell-1).
Take A=x^27 I_7+S_7+x^54 S_7^2 and b=1+x+x^6, with S_7 the forward
cyclic shift. Expand the polynomial entries as circulants in the convention
P^s_{i,j}=1 iff i=j+s. Then
  H_X=[ A_tilde | I_7 tensor B_tilde^T ],
  H_Z=[ I_7 tensor B_tilde | A_tilde^T ].
The binary CSS matrices have 882t columns and 441t rows each; H_X has
variable degree3 and check degree6. The stopping distance of H_X is EXACTLY SIX.
All new proofs, including the source-matrix, binary-coefficient, covering,
companion-row and CSS bridges, have checked Lean versions.

Moreover the actual unnormalized second squared singular value satisfies
  sigma_2(H_X)^2 >= 12.
Thus Proposition5.1's condition sigma_2(H_X)^2<gamma/2=3/2 is impossible
on this family. Whenever the strict classical gap sigma_2^2<18 holds, BOTH
Zhu–Chugg two-eigenvalue stopping-bound expressions are negative. Incidence
information certifies six even where these spectral expressions provide no
positive lower bound.

This is a theorem about explicit CSS check matrices and graph stopping sets.
It supplies no logical k(t), logical distance d(t), asymptotic rate, decoding
threshold or performance claim. In particular the size-six witness is an H_Z
row, hence a stabilizer support, not a weight-six logical operator. The t=1
matrix is exactly Example3.2 in the source's presentation of the reported
[[882,24,d]],18<=d<=24 code. Those logical parameters are SOURCE REPORTED;
our stopping theorem does not reprove them. Nonzero logical dimension for every
t is not asserted. This family does not meet the source spectral inequality.

DEFINITIONS AND SOURCE TRANSLATION

Rows are Check=Z/7 x Z/ell; variables are Bool x Check, with false denoting the
left block and true the right. In zero-based coordinates the column incidences
are:
  false(j,f): (j,f+27), (j+1,f), (j+2,f+54);
  true(j,f):  (j,f), (j,f-1), (j,f-6).
The three scalar circulants and the two block matrices are defined explicitly
as NATURAL matrices, then cast to F_2 for CSS and to R for the spectrum.
Nodup proofs ensure the natural coefficients are zero or one when63 divides ell.
HX_count/HX_incidence and HX2_incidence identify these actual matrices with the
incidence predicate. The source's n1=r1=7, r2=n2=1, gamma=3,k=6 and all
transpose signs are retained. No change to Panteleev–Kalachev's older B1 labeling
is silently assumed; we use Abiad–Castriota's displayed matrix.

A stopping set is NONEMPTY and each row's selected-neighbor cardinality is
unequal to one. This is the source's graph definition. It includes noncodeword
stopping sets and stabilizers, so classical distance, logical distance and
stopping distance are separate invariants. The real spectrum is the descending
ordered spectrum of H_X H_X^T, INCLUDING MULTIPLICITY. It is neither normalized
nor the spectrum of a constituent/protograph alone.

PROOFS AND THEIR LEAN INTERFACES

1. Sound extension test — IncidenceList.lean:
   survivesList_of_stopping.
Given a partial support S contained in a stopping set T, select a check with
exactly one neighbor in S. T must contain another neighbor of that check outside
S. Branch over every such check neighbor. If |T|<=|S|+fuel, one branch reaches T
before fuel is exhausted. The proof maintains S.Nodup and uses list length, not
a multiset count with possible duplicates. A rejected root therefore excludes
ALL stopping supersets in the stated size range. A successful branch is not used
as a lower-bound certificate. The checked picker proof proves degree one; row
completeness is checked against the actual incidences.

2. Two root orbits — IncidenceCore.lean:
   shift_incidence, stopping_image; Incidence.lean: running_lower_six.
Simultaneously translating both cyclic coordinates of variables and checks
preserves all incidences. A nonempty support has a selected variable; translate
that variable to (side,0,0). Both cardinality and stopping semantics are preserved.
There are only two side representatives. No support connectedness is assumed.

3. Finite certificate — Incidence.lean: normalized_roots_reject.
Lean kernel reduction verifies that both representatives are rejected with four
further additions, excluding all sizes1–5. The certificate uses ordinary lists
and exact modular arithmetic. It uses decide +kernel; no native_decide,
Lean.ofReduceBool, external SAT result, numerical eigensolver or Python premise
is used. Earlier quotient-sorting attempts were replaced by the checked list
implementation; unsuccessful scratch attempts are not part of this artifact.

4. Cover projection — IncidenceCover.lean: stopping_cover_image;
   IncidenceFamilyCore.lean: project_col, project_row, maps, lifts, local_inj;
   IncidenceFamily.lean: lower_six.
A graph cover projects a stopping support to a stopping support if incidences
map correctly, each target incident check lifts at each selected variable, and
the variable projection is injective on each check's neighbor set. The last
condition prevents two selected neighbors collapsing to a singleton. The theorem
proves this rather than assuming a stopping-distance conclusion.
Reduction Z/(63t)->Z/63 is a ring homomorphism. The explicit column lists map
onto the base columns; the six row-neighbor lists map onto six distinct base
neighbors. Hence all three cover hypotheses hold. A projected stopping support
has at least six variables and its cardinality is at most the original support's.
The abstract proof also accepts ell=0 (the infinite integer cover), but the
public finite-code family theorem requires t positive and makes no finite-code
claim at zero.

5. Upper witness, source and CSS — IncidenceFamilyCore.lean: six_stopping,
   six_card; IncidenceFamilySource.lean: six_is_HZ_row;
   IncidenceCSS.lean: A_circulant, D_circulant, css, HX2_incidence.
The six variables are
  false(0,0), false(0,-1), false(0,-6),
  true(0,27), true(1,0), true(2,54).
Each selected incidence has an explicitly checked partner on the opposite block.
The support has size six when63 divides ell, and it is exactly H_Z row(0,0).
For the base, Lean additionally checks the complete profile: every check has
induced degree0 or2 (six_profile).
For CSS, A and D=I_7 tensor B are circulants on the finite abelian product group.
Mathlib's circulant commutation theorem yields A D^T=D^T A. The explicitly
expanded binary product is A D^T+D^T A=0 in F_2. This is a proof of the actual
matrices' commutation, not a CSS assumption.

6. Degrees, dimensions and final contract — IncidenceDegrees.lean;
   IncidenceCertificate.lean: positive_family, source_B1_stopping_distance_six.
The final positive_family theorem combines exact matrix dimensions, degrees,
CSS commutation, the lower bound for ALL nonempty stopping supports of the
binary matrix, and a size-six upper witness. NeZero t is positivity for naturals.
The t=1 consumer is the source's882-column matrix. This aggregation also checks

7. Spectral obstruction and classical comparison — IncidenceSpectral.lean:
   plane_energy, second_lower_twelve, source_gap_impossible,
   zhu_chugg_bounds_negative.
Let a,b be arbitrary real coefficients on fiber-constant row-block indicators
at protograph rows0 and3. Their norm squared is ell(a^2+b^2). The first block
contributes3ell(a^2+b^2), since the relevant three column blocks are disjoint.
The second contributes9ell(a^2+b^2). Direct sums over the EXPANDED matrix give
energy=12 times norm squared. A linear functional recording the top eigenvector
coefficient has a nonzero kernel on this two-dimensional plane. The resulting
nonzero test vector has no top component, so its energy is at most sigma_2^2
 times its norm squared. Therefore sigma_2^2>=12. This uses no unproved DFT or
compressed-spectrum interlacing premise.

CLASSICAL AND SUBSEQUENT COMPARISON

Zhu and Chugg, Bounds on the Expansion Properties of Tanner Graphs,
IEEE TIT53(12),4834–4840,December2007, Corollary6, equations(41),(42):
https://hal.usc.edu/chugg/docs/pubs/zhu2007bounds.pdf
For binary regular incidence, N variables, column degree gamma, row degree k,
and eta the second Gram eigenvalue, their bit/parity stopping lower bounds are
  N(2gamma-eta)/(gamma k-eta),
  2N(2gamma+k-2-eta)/(k(gamma k-eta)).
Their usable strict spectral gap is eta<gamma k (unique top eigenvalue in the
connected regular setting). There is no separate small-support branch. They are
not the source's eta<gamma/2 criterion. Tanner's inspected26July2000 manuscript,
Theorems3.1 and4.1, gives the corresponding classical-distance bounds under
regular connected graph hypotheses; Zhu–Chugg explicitly extends the reasoning
to stopping sets. Their multi-eigenvector/irregular analysis is broader than the
two scalar formulas compared here, and is NOT refuted by their vacuity here.

In our family N=14ell,gamma=3,k=6,eta>=12. If eta<18, both denominators are
positive while6-eta<0 and10-eta<0. The displayed bounds are therefore negative,
proved in Lean for the ACTUAL eta. If that strict gap fails, these displayed
classical formulas are outside their strict-gap regime. No connectivity proof
or numerically inferred strict gap is needed to establish this dichotomy.
