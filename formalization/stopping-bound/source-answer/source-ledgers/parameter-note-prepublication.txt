PRECISE THEOREM
Take positive integers r,n1,ell,gamma, natural row degree k, m=r*ell>=2.
Let H=[A | I_r tensor B^T] be the real 0–1 expanded matrix with r*ell rows
and (n1+r)*ell columns, column degree gamma and row degree k. Source circulants
are included; the formal theorem does not require circulancy, so covers a larger
matrix class. s is the second-largest eigenvalue of HH^T, with multiplicity.
For a±=(m/gamma)(1±sqrt(1-2s/gamma)),

    NOT (s<gamma/2 AND a+>2).

If s<gamma/2, then r=1 and 1<a+<=2. Every nonempty stopping support S
has |S|>=2, a-<|S| and a+<=|S|. Thus the separate small-size branch is
excluded, but the resulting integer lower bound is exactly two.
Positive column degree alone already excludes singleton stopping sets.
Zero-width first factors, gamma=0 and matrices without a second singular value
are excluded explicitly. No girth, connectivity, monomial-entry, or desired
spectral-envelope premise is added. No logical-distance assertion is made.

PROOF
If r>=2, the repeated scalar block forces s>=gamma^2>=gamma/2. The expanded
matrix proof uses a two-dimensional fiber-constant plane and a vector orthogonal
to a top Gram eigenvector. The scalar block supplies energy gamma^2 times norm
squared; the other block contributes a nonnegative quantity.
If r=1, q=n1+1>=2, m=ell and edge counting gives k=q*gamma. Binary regularity
and the trace of HH^T give k(m-gamma)<=(m-1)s, with gamma<=m. Under the strict
gap this forces gamma>m-(m-1)/(2q)>3m/4. Checked radical algebra then yields
1<a+<=2 and 0<=a-<2. Positive columns give |S|>=2, excluding the small branch
and already implying the large-root conclusion. The parent note gives the
complete energy/trace/radical derivations, each matched to Lean.
