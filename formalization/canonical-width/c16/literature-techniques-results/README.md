# Literature review evidence

Read [the comparison and proposed next plan](../LITERATURE-TECHNIQUES.md).
This deliverable answers which inspected methods fit the C16 problem. It does
not answer whether the width is16 or18, launch another solver campaign, or
establish a speedup.

- `sources.json`: primary versions/hashes, theorem locators, applicability,
  question screens, bounded GitHub/X coverage and unreviewed leads.
- `diagnostic.json`: literal99,808-edge source graph and13-support cover counts.
- `replay_review.py`: cheap independent reconstruction of those diagnostics and
  source-question links. Run with Python3.10 or newer from any directory.
- `verification.json`: actual replay/validator outcomes, separate from the
  proposed future experiments.
- `manifest.json`: hashes of the review and its supporting evidence.
- `handoff.json`: preserved branch, ownership and publication state.

The three newly retrieved PDFs and reused SEA2025 HTML are cached in ignored
`local-extraction/literature` files. Primary URLs and their exact byte hashes
are recorded; this review does not redistribute their full texts. The replay
reports a missing source cache explicitly. It can still reconstruct the graph
from the checked-in source coordinates.

The13 family supports mean7 triples,5 quadruples and1 all-five case. Their169
ordered pairs, or91 representatives under the checked sector exchange, are a
cover of remaining possibilities, not169 known feasible cases. The previous
exclusion proofs justify this count; no new support is excluded here.

Publication through PR17 is pending its required review. No master integration
or new canonical C16/Lean completion is claimed.
