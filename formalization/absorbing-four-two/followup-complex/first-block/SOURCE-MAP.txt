Formal statements and evidence scope

PairRootBridge.lean
  PairRootBridge.corrected_count_identity
  PairRootBridge.count42_eq_corrected_energy
  General finite cubic/no-four-cycle incidence count. No CSS or cyclic premise.

GlobalCounting.lean
  GlobalCounting.count42
  GlobalCounting.counted_support_is_absorbing
  The counted four-subsets use elementarity and exactly two odd checks. Under
  cubicity/no four-cycles, strict-majority absorption follows. This bridges the
  computable count to the source's absorbing-set convention.

FourColumnClassification.lean, OrderedRoots.lean, PairRootBridge.lean
  FourColumnClassification.exact_local_multiplicity
  OrderedRoots.ordered_roots_eq_four_count42
  PairRootBridge.rootPairSum_eq_four_count42
  Local incidence classification, ordering/support correspondence, factor four.

PairEnergy.lean
  PairEnergy.missing_pair_energy_identity
  Exact integer incidence energy and ordered nonadjacent-pair identity.

FourCompletions.lean
  FourCompletions.proper_common_le_four
  General proof that each proper common-neighbor set has at most four elements.

CyclicNormalization.lean
  CyclicNormalization.cyclic_corrected_count_identity
  Explicit socket incidence model, equivariance, and free ordered-root
  normalization over finite additive groups. No odd/prime-lift restriction.

FiniteFourier.lean, SocketFourier.lean, SocketIncidence.lean
  AbsorbingFourier.parseval

  AbsorbingFourier.Fourier_energy

CyclicFourierCount.lean
  AbsorbingFourier.cyclic_Fourier_count_formula
  Combined corrected Fourier formula over a characteristic-zero field with
  star, assuming a faithful additive character with star(psi a)=psi(-a).
  Standard complex exponential/norm specialization remains a written bridge.

FanoBlock.lean

ProjectionObstruction.lean
  ProjectionObstruction.five_projected_edges / induced_degree_three / count42_zero
  A four-variable cubic/no-four-cycle induced projected diamond is not
  elementary. This checks the qualification needed in the normal-graph dictionary.

HGPIncidence.lean

  General proof that a mixed left/right product edge has no proper common
  neighbor, supporting the checked pure-type count reduction.

HGPCSS.lean

HGPSpectral.lean
  HGPSpectral.M_eq_gram / identical_all_evaluated_M / no_evaluated_M_only_count
  Actual rectangular evaluated matrices D_j=[A|beta_j I], their product M_j,
  and the impossibility of recovering these counts from all M_j alone.
  This works for any frequency scalars beta_j in a commutative star ring.

Larger concrete certificates
  FullQuantumAFast, FullQuantumBCert, FullQuantumBFast, QuantumRows,
  QuantumObstruction and SpectralObstruction have only the verification status
  explicitly recorded in verification.json. Their source files or candidate
  proof text alone must not be counted as completed checks.

SocketCompletion.lean, CompletionBridge.lean
  SocketCompletion.completions_correct / completions_unique
  CompletionBridge.candidates_eq_properCommon
  Modular completion from occupied sockets, uniqueness under no-four-cycles,
  and reconstruction of proper common neighbors from exclusive endpoint checks.

IncidenceEquivalence.lean, QuantumReindex.lean
  IncidenceEquivalence.count42_eq
  QuantumReindex.source_A_bridge / source_B_bridge
  QuantumReindex.literal_A_count / literal_B_count
  General count invariance under variable/check equivalences, the actual index
  equivalences between tensor and source constructions, and counts 49/35 for
  the explicit literal Fin 168 lookup tables. These supersede the unfinished
  large finite-reduction route above without promoting its candidate checks.

External finite computations
  reproduce.py, independent.py and the separate direct.cpp checker verify the
  full quantum examples, including ranks, connectedness and commutation.
  These exact computations are not formal rank/connectivity theorems.

Smaller generalized-bicycle witness
  GB48.lean: cubic/no-four-cycle conditions, normalized sums 32/12, counts
  192/72, and full integer Gram equality.
  GBReflection.lean: generic shifted reflection preserves Fourier magnitude.
  GB48Spectral.lean: actual evaluated socket matrices and M_j-only obstruction.
  Only successful modules listed in verification.json are certified; unfinished
  versions are explicitly separated as candidates. reproduce_gb48.py provides
  independent exact finite verification, including CSS/ranks/connectivity.
