# Re-replication citation coverage audit

The earlier replication uses 14 entries in [references.tex](../paper/references.tex). The first re-replication handoff passed four entries to the writer. That set omitted the R Core Team citation and the paper for the lmerTest implementation of the Satterthwaite approximation. The original delivery review checked the entries that were present. It did not establish coverage of the methods and software used.

The [citation inventory](../citation-inventory.json) maps the current methods and software to the body sections and bibliography keys that credit them. Its prior-reference reconciliation accounts for all 14 earlier entries. Nine are retained or updated, including two documentation entries now covered by the same SAS/STAT 15.2 manual. Five have a recorded reason for omission. The current paper also credits the original Satterthwaite paper, the two robustness criteria and the source for its Bonferroni adjustment.

- R Core Team, Rcpp, lme4, nlme, lmerTest, pbkrtest and Matrix bibliographic metadata were checked against the installed R `citation()` output. Package versions were checked against `packageDescription()`. Base R is formally cited, with a separate CRAN source archive link. The package links identify each tool's CRAN project.
- NumPy and SciPy citations were checked against the projects' [NumPy](https://numpy.org/citing-numpy/) and [SciPy](https://scipy.org/citing-scipy/) citation instructions. Python, PyYAML, PyMuPDF, Poppler and Tectonic use versioned software entries with official project URLs. PyMuPDF 2.1 supported source-figure extraction. The delivery review extractor is pinned separately to 1.26.6, as recorded in the [link inventory](../provenance/pdf-links.json). Poppler's version was verified with `pdftotext -v` in the rerun environment.
- The [lmerTest paper](https://doi.org/10.18637/jss.v082.i13) documents its implementation of Satterthwaite degrees of freedom. The [Satterthwaite article record](https://www.jstor.org/stable/3002019) identifies the underlying method's original publication. The two sources are credited together where the paper describes checking the study calculation against lmerTest.
- The [SAS/STAT 15.2 MIXED manual](https://go.documentation.sas.com/api/docsets/statug/15.2/content/mixed.pdf) documents the REML default and the denominator rules in its PROC MIXED and MODEL statement descriptions. This is documentation for the named methods. Kupzyk's dissertation is the evidence for what the original study used.
- The [Bradley publisher record](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.2044-8317.1978.tb00581.x) and [Serlin publication record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10937332/) were checked for bibliographic metadata. Kupzyk's p. 105 specifies the numerical robustness ranges applied in the re-replication. The dissertation page remains the direct source for those ranges. [Dunn](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1961.10482090) supports the simultaneous confidence interval procedure, with the 54-cell family specified by this study.

No simulation settings, numerical outputs or claims were changed for the citation correction.
