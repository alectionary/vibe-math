# Predeclared computational budget (2026-10-29 UTC)

A 54-cell one-draw pilot took median 0.0765 and mean 0.0963 seconds per lme4 fit (first package load included); 100 exemplary masks took 0.281 seconds. Four-core quota, 16 GiB RAM. Use three single-thread empirical workers; reserve one core for independent checks/report work. Planned main run: 400 draws ×54 cells, plus 11,600 baseline draws (12,000 total), 2,000 null draws each in first/last cells, and 200 independent dense-covariance-generator draws in four corner cells. Total 38,000 fits; expected about 61 CPU minutes /20 elapsed minutes with three workers, allowing roughly 40 minutes for contention/retries. Full original 1,382,400 fits is approximately37 CPU hours under this pilot; not attempted.

At p=.8, 400 draws have SE .020 and two-SE band .040 per cell; this cannot resolve subpercentage empirical cell differences. Baseline12000 has SE about.0031 at p=.867 and two-SE band .0062. Exemplary analysis uses1000 independent dropout masks per nonzero-attrition cell; its SD is mask variation, its SE is uncertainty in our mean, and comparison with the original single mask uses predictive variation rather than pretending the original is a population mean. Threshold checks use200 masks per J.

All empirical batches have deterministic independent seeds and save100-draw checkpoints. Boundary variance estimates are retained; genuine convergence problems retry nloptwrap and are counted if unresolved. Primary summaries show failed draws and conservative unconditional rejection rates as well as rates among converged draws. No long simulation began before this budget was recorded.
