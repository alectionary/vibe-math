WHY THE CLASSICAL ANSWER APPLIES

For four selected variables, zero binary syndrome means every selected check
has even degree. A degree-four check would force all other selected checks to
be private, by the no-four-cycle condition. That contradicts their even degrees
and the column degree of three. Thus all selected checks have degree two,
which is exactly a Pasch configuration. Every selected variable has three even
checks and zero odd checks, so the absorbing-majority condition holds.

This is classical even-configuration/codeword mathematics, explicitly discussed
for Steiner designs by Fujiwara2014. Our generic proof does not need a complete
design. The equality to the weight-four coefficient gives both the classical
MacWilliams count and a simple standard pair-XOR counting route. We do not
claim that general weight-four counting is a newly solved open problem.

THE EXPLICIT CYCLIC FORMULA

At an ordered adjacent root pair (u,v), let W be the proper common neighbors:
third variables completing genuine six-cycles at that root, excluding a single
check shared by all three. Let t=|W|. For each check c let k_c count variables
of W incident with c. Define E=sum_c k_c^2, and let F=sum_{c in H(u) union H(v)}
k_c^2. Every w in W has exactly one check outside the root checks. It follows
that E-F-t counts ordered outer pairs producing genuine Pasches. Each Pasch
has24root/order choices, giving

  24 N40 = sum_{ordered adjacent roots} (E-F-t).

For a cyclic lift of size N>0, normalize roots to (p,0),(q,d). Let D_j be the
full rectangular evaluated incidence matrix and b_hat_W its proper-common-
neighbor indicator transformed with the same unnormalized negative-exponent
DFT as the earlier complex bridge. Then

  N40 = (N/24) sum_{normalized adjacent roots}
           [(1/N)sum_j ||D_j b_hat_W(j)||^2 -F-t].

The artifact proves the actual-support/root correspondence, the factor24,
cyclic normalization, matrix-vector interpretation, standard complex character,
sign and Euclidean norm/Parseval factor. It applies to every positive N,
including1 and even/composite lift sizes. No check-regularity or complete-
Steiner-design premise was smuggled into the general theorem.

The expression uses more than M_j=D_jD_j*. Both W and the correction F preserve
which variables meet which checks. A projected K4 can come from one degree-four
check and fail to be a Pasch; a checked small counterexample illustrates this.
