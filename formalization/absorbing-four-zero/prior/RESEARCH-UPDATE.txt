RESULT

The standard-complex bridge is now a general Lean proof. For every positive
integer N, the checked formula uses the actual character

  psi(a) = exp(2*pi*i*a.val/N),    omega = exp(2*pi*i/N).

It proves injectivity through mathlib's standard character, the conjugation
identity star(psi(a))=psi(-a), unit norm, and the Fourier convention

  psi(-j*a) = omega^(-j.val*a.val).

The exponent on the right is an ordinary integer, so this statement also checks
the wraparound convention for even/composite lift sizes. N=1 is included.

For socket incidence H(p,a)={(r,a+e):(r,e) in sockets(p)}, define the full
rectangular evaluated matrix and transformed indicator by

  D_j[r,p] = sum_(r,e in sockets(p)) omega^(-j*e),
  b_hat_j[p] = sum_(p,a in W) omega^(-j*a).

The formalization connects their ordinary matrix-vector product to the existing
finite convolution theorem, and connects its Euclidean complex-vector norm to
the algebraic energy. Thus the checked source formula is

  N42(H) = (N/4) sum_roots [t^2+2t - (1/N) sum_j ||D_j b_hat_j||^2].

Roots are ordered central-pair representatives ((p,0),(q,d)) with distinct
variables sharing a check. W is their proper common-neighbor set: a third
variable forms a genuine six-cycle with the pair, using distinct check nodes.
t=|W|. The hypotheses remain exactly column degree three and no four-cycles;
check degrees may vary. Every elementary (4,2) support is automatically absorbing
under these hypotheses, by the previously checked classification. This is a
count of four-variable supports, not labeled drawings or cycle occurrences.

The proof does not replace D_j by eigenvalues or by M_j=D_j D_j*. The incidence
correction is essential. The preceding block proved why projected diamonds alone
can include non-elementary check incidences and why full M_j data lose information.

THE EXPLICIT QUANTUM WITNESS

The new socket-to-matrix theorem connects this complex interpretation to the
saved 168-column matrices, through the existing tensor/literal index equivalences.
The right sockets are {0,-1,-3}; their evaluation is

  beta_j = 1+omega^j+omega^(3j),

which checks the positive exponent caused by the transposed binary right block.
The full rectangular matrix is exactly [A | beta_j I]. The two saved A/A'
examples have identical full complex M_j at every frequency, and their exact
elementary (4,2) counts are 49 and 35. Both counts, their actual binary/source
interpretation, the complex evaluated matrices, and the failure of any M_j-only

LITERATURE FINDINGS

The complete Steiner-triple-system special case was already solved in 1995.
Grannell, Griggs and Mendelsohn's C14 configuration is exactly the elementary
(4,2) pattern when triples become variables and points become checks. Their
formula is N42=v(v-1)(v-3)/4-6P, where P counts Pasch configurations. Figure 2
and the derivation were inspected in the author-hosted accepted preprint.
https://grannell.net/Papers/smallbasis.pdf

Heinlein-Ostergard's Theorem 3 (2021 preprint; 2022 journal) also establishes
O(v^3) counting of every variable four-line STS configuration, using the earlier
reduction. Efficient counting in that special case is established prior work.
https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/jcd.21839

The distinction from general socket data is substantive: a complete STS covers
every check-point pair exactly once. General cubic/no-four-cycle incidences only
cover each pair at most once. Beezer's general t-design reduction was checked in
the relevant indexed primary-preprint passages. A check-regular cubic incidence
structure is a 1-design, but C14 is itself a generator at t=1; the reduction for
non-generators does not compute it from other generator counts. For a complete
STS, t=2 and C14 is a non-generator. The finite shape/hypothesis distinction is
checked in ClassicalConfiguration.lean. The final journal PDF subsequently opened
through the normal publisher browser's open-archive link. Its download stalled,
so the full final-text comparison remains incomplete; the ledger names the
preprint passages actually read.
https://doi.org/10.1006/jcta.2001.3185

General expanded-graph ETS enumeration was already established, and the newer
Pacenti-Raveendran-Vasic detector-model work also uses exhaustive dpl search.
Our result is not the first algorithm capable of counting these supports on an
expanded graph. P34's base-pattern counts and lifting results were also compared
with the full target; their stated counts concern different objects.

https://arxiv.org/html/2606.11987v1
