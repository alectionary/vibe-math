\begin{abstract}
We could not establish the full dissertation findings of \citet{quintavalle2022}, who reported oracle-assisted decoding guarantees, logical gate constructions, and confinement-based single-shot correction. We completed 0 of 4 sustainable thresholds, 0 of 2 capacity analyses, 0 of 2 suppression fits, and 0 of 3 random-product performance analyses. Finite support includes 1,061 guaranteed-range phase-error checks, 256 seed pairs, 19 matrix comparisons, 560,000 reconstructed trials and 80,000 original-software diagnostic trials. The thesis text was read and the rendered headline pages were inspected; finite tests do not prove universal results.

\section{Introduction}
We could not establish the full dissertation findings of \citet{quintavalle2022}: the finite checks support parts of the decoding and gate constructions, but universal theorems and production estimates are unresolved. We completed 0 of 4 sustainable threshold estimates, 0 of 2 code-capacity analyses, 0 of 2 suppression fits, and 0 of 3 random-product performance analyses. The published sustainable thresholds are 3.08(4)\% and 2.90(1)\% for surface codes, and 2.90(2)\% and 2.78(2)\% for toric codes, in MWPM+BP/OSD and BP/OSD-twice order. There are no corresponding fitted values from this audit. Missing comparisons are not evidence of nonreplication.

The scientific question is which parts of the dissertation survive independent finite checks, and which require evidence beyond those checks. A hypergraph-product (HGP) quantum code is built from classical parity-check matrices; its parameters $[[n,k,d]]$ give physical qubits, logical qubits, and distance. Single-shot correction uses one noisy syndrome measurement per correction step. An assertion about an entire code family is stronger than an exact calculation on a small member of that family. The inventory in Appendix A retains all 41 claims across the six chapters and associated appendices; inventory completeness is not theorem verification.

\section{What we did ourselves, and what we took from others}
Our contribution is an independent finite implementation and comparison, not a new universal proof. We computed binary matrix ranks, kernels, logical bases, decoder outcomes and phase-sensitive gate images; built independent tensor and computational-basis checks; and ran reconstructed Monte Carlo experiments. The original work supplies the code definitions, conditional theorems, gate constructions, simulation designs and published numbers \citep{quintavalle2022}. We distinguish the original software from our input settings: the former is pinned to an upstream revision, while missing source grids and depth choices make our runs diagnostic.

The full thesis PDF was read as text, and the rendered pages that support the headline and Published entries were inspected. Quotations follow that reading. Printed figures and equations on those pages were inspected. Sign conventions and equality-versus-bound readings that those figures do not settle remain provisional. Published theorem statements, numerical observations and conjectures are separate kinds of evidence throughout this paper.

\section{Finite decoding and logical-operation findings}
The decoding checks support the guaranteed finite regime and reproduce unfavorable toric scaling on a reconstructed grid. Chapter 3 Theorem 1 reported correction of errors of weight up to $(d-1)/2$ with classical seed and transposed-seed decoder oracles, $O(k)$ oracle calls and $O(n^2)$ additional operations (PDF p.44). The same article's abstract reported weight smaller than $(d-1)/2$. This audit follows the displayed theorem. The difference is not treated as an established error. Our 1,061 guaranteed-range phase-error checks all pass. Exhaustive classification includes 8,192 planar3 and 256 toric2 patterns; it is not a claim that all those patterns correct successfully. The canonical decomposition and logical line-basis checks pass for all 256 binary $2\times2$ seed pairs and six named examples.

The stochastic experiment supplies 560,000 reconstructed trials over 56 settings. At phase-flip probability .03, failure rates are .0596, .0623, .0714 and .1828 for distances 6, 8, 10 and 20. This is consistent with the source's toric anti-threshold observation (Chapter 3 Figure 4, PDF p.52), but does not establish an exact reproduction of its curves or tie conventions. The source describes random (3,4) scaling as less clear: the pseudo-threshold appears to increase, there is no common crossing, and a toric-like adverse trend is expected at larger distances (PDF p.52, lines 2644--2649). We did not run this family because its original seeds and grid were not recovered. Results and intervals are in \href{https://github.com/alectionary/vibe-math/blob/master/formalization/quintavalle-hgp/results/toric_noise.json}{\path{results/toric_noise.json}}; the decoder and interval functions are \href{https://github.com/alectionary/vibe-math/blob/master/formalization/quintavalle-hgp/analysis/toric_noise.py#L15}{\path{analysis/toric_noise.py}} and \href{https://github.com/alectionary/vibe-math/blob/master/formalization/quintavalle-hgp/analysis/toric_noise.py#L39}{\path{analysis/toric_noise.py}}.

Logical basis and ideal gate checks support selected finite constructions, while noisy fault tolerance is untested. Square Hadamard--SWAP and square CZ--S pass phase-sensitive checks on planar3, planar5, toric2, toric3, Hamming65 and Hamming98. The latter two codes have 17 and 32 logical qubits. All nine symmetric-code appendix rows agree in construction parameters; quantum distance uses the source theorem rather than an independent exhaustive proof. There are 361 collision-free round-robin schedules and 256 successful ideal logical CZ pair actions. The source's intermediate ReShape correction (Chapter 4, Section 4.1, PDF pp.70--71) is a condition of the fault-tolerance construction, not something established by these ideal checks. Conventional ideal S and T injection gadgets pass both branches with conditional phase correction. The rendered round-robin figure (PDF p.71) and the S/T injection circuits (PDF p.72) were inspected; noisy injection was not run. Evidence is in \href{https://github.com/alectionary/vibe-math/blob/master/formalization/quintavalle-hgp/results/exact_checks.json}{\path{results/exact_checks.json}} and \href{https://github.com/alectionary/vibe-math/blob/master/formalization/quintavalle-hgp/results/independent_checks.json}{\path{results/independent_checks.json}}.

\section{Confinement and single-shot findings}
The finite confinement checks do not prove a stochastic threshold. Chapter 5 Theorem 1 requires a quantum low-density parity-check (LDPC) family with good confinement and distance $d\geq an^b$ for positive $a,b$. It establishes adversarial single-shot correction; good X confinement yields the phase-Z statement. Theorem 2 additionally requires bounded qubit degree and good linear confinement for its local-stochastic threshold. Theorem 3 states X confinement with $t$ equal to Z distance and $f(x)=x^3/4$ or better for 3D products (PDF p.86). Our 12-qubit finite example enumerates 4,096 physical errors and passes 31,232 residual checks. We did not independently re-prove these theorems. The proposed extension from general good confinement to a local-stochastic threshold is explicitly conjectural, as is clear from the text preceding Theorem 2.

The independent tensor construction matches all 19 available archived Hx/metacheck matrices entry for entry. This validates finite matrix construction, not the absent production fits. The original BP+OSD twice decoder (belief propagation followed by ordered-statistics decoding at both stages) ran eight diagnostic settings of 10,000 trials each. At $L=5$, data noise $p=.1$, syndrome noise $q=.03$ and reconstructed depth $N=1$, repair reduces failures from 617 to 72 per 10,000. This supports improvement in our diagnostic; the source's over-an-order-of-magnitude improvement refers to its high-syndrome-noise experiment (Chapter 5 Figure 2, PDF p.94), whose exact depth/grid was not recovered. Our final round injects fresh data noise before perfect measurement. \href{https://github.com/alectionary/vibe-math/blob/master/formalization/quintavalle-hgp/results/original_diagnostic_grid.json}{\path{results/original_diagnostic_grid.json}} records these outcomes and settings.

The production comparisons are unavailable. Chapter 5 Table I reports a toric phase-flip capacity threshold of 21.55(1)\% for BP+OSD versus a cited sweep threshold of 15.5\%; the 6.05 percentage-point contrast is a source comparison, not our experiment. Appendix E reports suppression exponents 1.91(3) at $N=0$ and 1.15(3) at $N=8$, a difference of .76. Table III reported $[[1336,4,6]]$, $[[3100,5,8]]$ and $[[5964,6,10]]$ random-product codes. A sentence on the same page reported that the distance-10 instance encodes ten logical qubits; the Published cell follows the table, and the difference is not treated as an established error. Figure 4 reported a suggested threshold at 2.7\% after 16 rounds (PDF pp.95--96). None of those three performance analyses was completed. The sustainable-threshold parent retains its surface and toric contrasts, .18 and .12 percentage points, each with a .01 rounding tolerance; no pooled effect is justified.

\section{Assumptions}
\subsection{Reconstructed grid and decoder ties}
We supplied a 56-setting toric grid with 10,000 trials per point and random seed 20261103. Distances 6, 8, 10 and 20 follow the identifiable source design; exact probabilities, coordinate complements and tie conventions are reconstructed. Half the draws use uniform random numbers and half binomial draws. Independent decoder agreement is checked away from ties, and valid syndrome recovery is checked throughout. The scope is recorded in \href{https://github.com/alectionary/vibe-math/blob/master/formalization/quintavalle-hgp/results/toric_noise.json}{\path{results/toric_noise.json}}.
\subsection{Diagnostic depth and source software}
We supplied depth $N=1$ for the eight repair diagnostics because the exact Figure 2 setup was unavailable. Four pilot conditions test feasibility rather than estimate thresholds. We retain the original final-round data-noise behavior and separate metacode failures from logical failures. These choices prevent the diagnostics from being counted as source-equivalent fits.
\subsection{Rounding tolerances}
We use the sum of source endpoint half-units for derived contrasts: .055 percentage points for capacity and .01 for suppression. Each sustainable-threshold topology contrast has .01 percentage-point tolerance. These are fixed source-resolution bounds, not margins inferred from our outcomes; no comparison can be resolved without corresponding estimates. Non-scalar claims have no fabricated numerical tolerance.

\section{Ambiguities that were resolved}
\subsection{Independent matrix ordering}
The tensor construction agrees with all 19 archived Hx/metacheck matrices, resolving the finite ordering comparison for the available inputs. \href{https://github.com/alectionary/vibe-math/blob/master/formalization/quintavalle-hgp/results/independent_checks.json}{\path{results/independent_checks.json}} contains each topology, size and match indicator; \href{https://github.com/alectionary/vibe-math/blob/master/formalization/quintavalle-hgp/analysis/independent_checks.py#L25}{\path{analysis/independent_checks.py}} implements the independent tensor construction. This does not recover absent surface-L11 inputs.
\subsection{Support versus phase in sibling CZ}
Independent computational-basis phase checks reproduce negative stabilizer images from the symplectic calculation. Hamming98 has 16 negative images; toric2 also exhibits phase failures. A supplied Z Pauli correction restores the tested signs, but changes the gate and does not replicate the literal source expression. \href{https://github.com/alectionary/vibe-math/blob/master/formalization/quintavalle-hgp/analysis/independent_checks.py#L73}{\path{analysis/independent_checks.py}} and \href{https://github.com/alectionary/vibe-math/blob/master/formalization/quintavalle-hgp/results/independent_checks.json}{\path{results/independent_checks.json}} document this distinction. Whether the source uses an implicit Pauli frame is unresolved.

\section{What remains unresolved}
The main unresolved comparisons require source inputs or proofs, rather than more interpretations of the finite successes. The following six limits delimit the evidence.
\paragraph{Limit 1.} The thesis PDF was read and the rendered headline pages, including Chapter 4 figures on PDF pages 67--72, were inspected. Phase conventions and missing numerical grids remain provisional.
\paragraph{Limit 2.} Finite binary checks do not prove the dissertation universal theorems or noisy fault tolerance.
\paragraph{Limit 3.} Original stochastic grids, tie conventions, random seeds, MWPM implementation and surface L11 code were not recovered.
\paragraph{Limit 4.} 0 of 4 sustainable thresholds, 0 of 2 capacity analyses, 0 of 2 suppression fits, and 0 of 3 random-code performance analyses were completed.
\paragraph{Limit 5.} Toric L11 pilot completed 9 trials in 180 seconds: approximately 55.6 CPU-hours per 10000-trial point; full production grids are unresolved.
\paragraph{Limit 6.} Sibling gate and partition discrepancies are candidate source-comparison gaps. The printed figures were inspected; the phase convention is not settled by those figures. They are not established source errors.

The candidate gate discrepancies have several causes still in contention: extraction or convention differences, implementation error, and an error in the source statement. The independent phase representation reduces concern about a single phase implementation, while truly symmetric matrices pass sibling Hadamard--SWAP checks that fail 18 support checks for weak image symmetry on toric3. Exact Z-sector partition distances 3, 2 and 3 for planar3, toric2 and toric3 differ from a literal extracted floor-half-distance expression. The evidence does not rank a source error above an unsettled phase convention. The equation images were inspected. Explicit symmetry and Pauli-frame conventions remain the highest-priority discriminating artifacts; X-sector and universal partition statements remain unverified.

The numerical stopping evidence is concrete: the toric-L11 pilot completed nine trials in 180 seconds, projecting about 55.6 CPU-hours per 10,000-trial point before complete grids. Surface-L11 inputs, the MWPM implementation, exact stochastic grids and random seeds were not recovered. \href{https://github.com/alectionary/vibe-math/blob/master/formalization/quintavalle-hgp/results/original_pilots.json}{\path{results/original_pilots.json}} gives each attempted condition and outcome. Resource limits explain uncompleted estimates but do not validate them. There is no complete gate architecture demonstration, and nonlinear confinement must not be conflated with the proved conditional linear-confinement theorem.
