\begin{abstract}\small
We partly replicated Quintavalle's dissertation findings that product codes admit adversarial decoding, logical gates and confinement-based single-shot correction \citep{quintavalle2022}: finite tests support several constructions, but the whole-dissertation replication is incomplete. We completed 0 of 4 sustainable threshold estimates, 0 of 2 capacity analyses, 0 of 2 suppression fits, and 0 of 3 random-family analyses. Published threshold advantages are 0.18 and 0.12 percentage points; our fitted advantages are not established. Published capacity and suppression contrasts are 6.05 percentage points and 0.76; ours are not established. Exact finite checks and 560,000 reconstructed ReShape trials provide limited support, not universal proofs or complete source-design reproduction. Source-ink and gate-convention gaps prevent interpreting candidate gate discrepancies as established source errors.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scientific question and principal findings}
We partly replicated the dissertation's findings, with finite support and an incomplete whole-dissertation replication. The exact completion counts are 0 of 4 sustainable threshold estimates, 0 of 2 capacity analyses, 0 of 2 suppression fits, and 0 of 3 random-family analyses. The question is whether hypergraph product constructions support reliable decoding, logical operations and correction with noisy measurements. The evidence answers some finite construction questions, while leaving the production performance and universal guarantees unestablished.

The published numerical targets are heterogeneous. Chapter 5 Table II reports surface thresholds of 3.08(0.04)\% and 2.90(0.01)\%, and toric thresholds of 2.90(0.02)\% and 2.78(0.02)\%, for matching plus belief propagation with ordered statistics decoding (BP+OSD) versus BP+OSD twice. Their differences are 0.18 and 0.12 percentage points; the two topologies have no justified pooled contrast. Table I gives toric capacity 21.55(0.01)\% versus a cited sweep-decoder value of 15.5\%; the 6.05-point contrast is inherited from that source comparison, not a new evaluation of sweep decoding. The text reports approximately 21.6\% for each capacity family. Appendix E gives suppression exponents 1.91(0.03) and 1.15(0.03), a difference of 0.76 \citep[Ch.~5, Tables I--II, Fig.~3, Appendix E]{quintavalle2022}. None has a corresponding fitted estimate here.

\section{What we did ourselves, and what we took from others}
Our contribution is an independent finite computational audit and reconstructed simulation, not a new universal proof. The dissertation supplies the constructions, theorem statements, designs and published results. Original C++ single-shot code supplies BP+OSD behavior; our wrapper supplies reconstructed settings and timed stopping. Independent binary linear algebra, sparse tensor construction and computational-basis phase calculations check different representations of the same objects.

The finite decoding census passes 1,061 below-half-distance phase errors on six named instances and classifies all 8,192 planar3 and 256 toric2 patterns. Failures outside the guarantee are retained. All 256 ordered pairs of binary 2-by-2 seeds pass the finite logical-basis check. All nine symmetric-code rows agree in length, dimension, seed distance and maximum stabilizer weight; quantum distance is inherited from the cited theorem, not exhaustively established. These are our calculations in \href{https://github.com/alectionary/vibe-math/blob/master/formalization/quintavalle-hgp/results/exact_checks.json}{\nolinkurl{results/exact_checks.json}}.

The independent 3D construction matches all 19 available original check and metacheck matrices entry for entry. The 12-qubit surface instance has 4,096 error words and 128 syndrome classes; 31,232 syndrome/noise checks pass the finite residual-bound diagnostic. These finite checks do not establish the general confinement theorems or stochastic threshold. Evidence is in \href{https://github.com/alectionary/vibe-math/blob/master/formalization/quintavalle-hgp/results/independent_checks.json}{\nolinkurl{results/independent_checks.json}} and \href{https://github.com/alectionary/vibe-math/blob/master/formalization/quintavalle-hgp/results/exact_checks.json}{\nolinkurl{results/exact_checks.json}}.

\section{Logical operations and provisional discrepancies}
The square gates pass phase-sensitive finite stabilizer checks, while sibling gates require source review. Literal sibling Hadamard-SWAP fails 18 support checks for the cyclic three-bit repetition seed under the weaker equality-of-images condition. Truly symmetric matrices remove that support failure. Literal sibling CZ produces 16 negative stabilizer images in the 98-qubit Hamming construction; independent computational-basis phases confirm those signs. A Z-Pauli correction repairs the signs for symmetric seeds but changes the implemented gate. These are candidate source-comparison gaps, not established author errors.

The diagonal-twin Z-sector distances are 3, 2 and 3 for planar3, toric2 and toric3. Treating the extracted floor-distance expression as a lower bound rather than equality changes the comparison; the source ink and full sector conventions are not certified. Separately, 361 round-robin support-size schedules and 256 ideal logical-pair actions pass. Neither noisy intermediate correction nor a fault-tolerant gate threshold was tested. Two ideal injection branches are related diagnostics, not a certification of image-only source wiring. \href{https://github.com/alectionary/vibe-math/blob/master/formalization/quintavalle-hgp/results/independent_checks.json}{\nolinkurl{results/independent_checks.json}} records the independent checks.

\section{Assumptions}
\subsection{Reconstructed probability grid and generators}
We supplied a toric grid and random seed because original simulation inputs were not recovered. Equal generator allocation is our diagnostic choice. Off-tie agreement between generic binary decoding and batched recurrence does not settle even-distance tie or complement conventions.
\subsection{Finite scope in place of universal enumeration}
We restrict algebraic checks to named instances and a complete small seed census. Finite success supports implementation consistency, not general correctness, asymptotic complexity or hardware scalability.
\subsection{Reconstructed single-shot depth}
We used one noisy cycle for the failure-mode grid and eight cycles for timing pilots. These settings do not establish the source's production design. Original surface and toric BP defaults are retained, rather than silently standardized.

\section{Ambiguities that were resolved}
\subsection{Sparse rank calculation}
A rank discrepancy arose because our sparse representation treated explicitly stored zeros as ones. Removing explicit zeros restores agreement on all 19 archived instances. This was our implementation defect, resolved by the independent matrix comparison in \href{https://github.com/alectionary/vibe-math/blob/master/formalization/quintavalle-hgp/results/independent_checks.json}{\nolinkurl{results/independent_checks.json}}.
\subsection{Reduced error weight}
The residual-bound check uses minimum weight among all errors with the same syndrome, as defined in the source. Minimization only modulo stabilizers would answer a different question. The 4,096-word census implements the same-syndrome definition and underlies the 31,232 finite checks.
\subsection{Support preservation versus phase preservation}
A support-only gate test is insufficient. Independent basis-state phases reproduce the sibling-CZ sign failures, so a single Pauli conjugation implementation is not the leading remaining explanation. Source ink, coordinate conventions and Pauli-frame conventions still require review.

\section{What remains unresolved}
The largest source gap is the unavailable original PDF ink. Browser text covers 122 pages, but PDF-byte download and screenshots failed under this environment's access policy. The first settling artifact is thesis PDF pages 66--68 (zero-based), followed by gate and Pauli-frame conventions or original implementation. Text extraction or convention mismatch ranks ahead of an established source error because these checks are missing. A later author HTML version corroborates text but cannot replace source-page inspection.

The largest numerical gap combines missing inputs and measured cost. Surface size 11, random-code seeds, raw figure grids, fitting notebooks and the matching first-stage implementation were not recovered. Surface and toric size-5 eight-cycle pilots completed ten trials in 0.165 and 0.366 seconds, respectively. Toric size 11 completed nine trials before a 180-second cap. At that throughput, one 10,000-trial point costs approximately 55.6 CPU-hours, before larger cycle counts, full grids or the source's minimum 25-failure rule. This is an approximate throughput extrapolation, dependent on error rate, not a full resource budget. At most two CPU processes ran concurrently. Unbounded production runs were stopped with missing inputs unresolved; pilots cannot be substituted for fitted thresholds. \href{https://github.com/alectionary/vibe-math/blob/master/formalization/quintavalle-hgp/results/original_pilots.json}{\nolinkurl{results/original_pilots.json}} records attempts and outcomes.

Universal proof gaps are separate from computational cost. Chapter 3 complement, homology and correctness appendices received finite construction checks. Chapter 4 triangular reduction was checked through independently computed line pairing. Chapter 5 percolation, inheritance and expander arguments were inventoried but not independently re-proved or formalized. The nonlinear-confinement stochastic question and complete scalable architecture remain open within this replication.

\section{Next steps}
Recover the thesis source pages and gate conventions first, then rerun support and phase comparisons under verified coordinates. Obtain the surface size-11 matrices, matching implementation, original random seeds and full numerical grid before spending the measured production budget. Refit all four sustainable thresholds, both capacity analyses and both suppression exponents, retaining the original denominators and uncertainty rules. Formal proofs require a separate mathematical argument or formalization; larger finite tests alone cannot settle them.
