## Written proof and semantic checks

The gradient at axis a and vertex v is u(v) XOR u(v+a). A plaquette boundary sums four such edges; its four corner bits each occur twice, hence zero. Coordinate shifts commute definitionally. A zero gradient forces adjacent vertex bits equal, so induction in x, then y, then z makes the field constant. Under first-axis restriction, the vertex formula is e(x,y,z) XOR e(x-1,y,z); shifting x forward gives the exact periodic chain difference. XY and XZ faces become y/z differences, and YZ faces become zero. Applying the already checked cyclic constancy theorem in the remaining coordinates yields the plane result. The syndrome-classification proof uses XOR linearity plus this kernel characterization.
