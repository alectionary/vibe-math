LIFT-LABEL ELIMINATION: PRECISE STATEMENTS AND PROOFS
Project-derived follow-up RQ-F16-lift-label-elimination, parent L-F16-other-absorbing.
All counts refer to unordered supports, not ordered embeddings or decoder events.

1. Objects and classification
Let H:V -> finite subsets of C have column size three and |H(u) intersect H(v)|<=1
for distinct variables. A four-variable elementary(4,0) support has every used
check incident with exactly two selected variables. Its six used checks are in
bijection with the six pairs of selected variables: the normal graph is K4.
Conversely such a Pasch is elementary(4,0). A zero binary syndrome four-support
is automatically elementary: odd check degrees are excluded; a degree-four
check would already use the unique common check of every pair and prevent the
remaining two sockets on every variable from being paired. These general facts
are in the preserved PaschClassification/PaschKernelCount formalization.

2. Ordinary monomial cover and projection
For N>=1, z(v,c) in Z/N on every existing edge, define
H_z(v,t)={(c,t+z(v,c)):c in H(v)}.
Two distinct fibers of the same base variable cannot share a check, since the
unique edge to any base check gives t+z=s+z only if t=s. Hence the pair-complete
four variables of a lifted Pasch have distinct base types. Their base types are
pair-complete. If three of these types contained the same base check c, base
linearity would force their pair checks to project to c. At one variable, its
single edge to c then forces the three lifted variables to share the same check,
contradicting elementary selected degree<=2. Thus all projected used checks
have selected degree<=2; cubicity and pair completeness give a base Pasch.
This excludes both repeated-type and false projected K4 configurations.
Evidence: general Lean, PaschLiftProjection.zero_syndrome_projects.

3. Exact voltage criterion and count
Fix a base Pasch with its variables ordered v0<v1<v2<v3 (choose any fixed order
on a finite base), and its unique pair checks c_ij. Put
 d_ij=z(vi,c_ij)-z(vj,c_ij), i<j.
Offsets x_i give the lifted pair incidences precisely when x_j-x_i=d_ij.
All six equations hold iff
 d01+d12=d02, d01+d13=d03, d02+d23=d03.                 (B)
Necessity is cancellation. Under(B), all solutions, uniquely, are
 (t,t+d01,t+d02,t+d03), t in Z/N.
For any solution the six projected pair checks were distinct, so the actual
lifted checks are distinct, and this is a Pasch. Distinct t give distinct
unordered supports because the base variables are distinct; different base
Pasches also have different projected supports. Section2 proves surjectivity.
Consequently
 N40(H_z)=N * #{base Pasches satisfying(B)}.            (1)
General Lean: PaschVoltage proves the solution equivalence/count over any
additive commutative group (cyclic count for all positive N). PaschLiftCriterion
connects the actual cover incidence and binary even-check condition to the six
offset equations. Projection is separately Lean checked. The final finite-set
partition and passage from fixed ordered base representatives to unordered
supports in(1) are the written bijection above, not a claimed Lean count theorem.
This is a specialization of classical voltage/cycle-consistency principles,
not a newly discovered elimination method. Eliminate at least one equation of
(B) for every base Pasch. At N=1 no base Pasch can be eliminated.

6. Full scalar-right QC-GHP incidence and obstruction
Over R_N=F2[x]/(x^N-1), let A_cv=x^z(v,c) on base edges and0 elsewhere, and
b=1+x+x^3. Set X=[A | b* I_C], Z=[b I_V | A*], where star transposes matrices
and replaces x by x^-1. XZ*=Ab*+b*A=0. With ordinary binary circulant expansion,
X left(v,t) has checks(c,t+z_vc), X right(c,t) has(c,t-e), e=0,1,3;
Z left(v,t) has(v,t+e), Z right(c,t) has(v,t-z_vc).
Assume b's binary columns have size3 and no pair intersects twice. Then X,Z
also have column3, row6 for these square cubic bases, and no Tanner4cycles:
left-left follows cover linearity, right-right follows the scalar factor, and
left-right intersects at most once since a left column has one edge per c.

No mixed Pasch exists, even for arbitrary z. Four pairwise adjacent variables:
- One left/three right: right-right adjacency forces all three right variables
  into the same check-type fiber c. The left has only one check of type c,
  forcing selected degree4 there, impossible.
- Two left/two right: the two right variables have the same type c. Each left
  must meet both at its unique c-check, forcing selected degree at least3.
- Three left/one right: the three lefts meet the right at its three distinct
  checks of the same type c. They have distinct base variable types; otherwise
  they could not be adjacent. All three base types contain c, so base linearity
  forces every left-left intersection to have type c. Their distinct c-checks
  then prevent adjacency, a contradiction.
An all-right Pasch is contained in one type fiber. Thus
 N40(X)=N*j_A+|C|*N40(B), N40(Z)=N*j_Astar+|V|*N40(B). (5)
Here B is the ordinary scalar circulant; transpose/reindexing does not change
its support count. Equation(5) is a written arbitrary-voltage extension; the
prior Lean HGP product count assumed constant left labels, and is not silently
reused for this stronger scope. The finite witnesses below are checked directly.
For N=7, B is Fano incidence and N40(B)=7. Both ten-type full Tanner graphs
therefore retain at least70 supports regardless of left labels. Left-only
elimination is possible, full elimination with that fixed factor is impossible.

checks degrees/linearity/commutation, and uses a different Gaussian elimination.
The zero-based support [28,42,70,84,98,112] is a weight-six word in ker X;

Rank proof over the quotient ring, including repeated-root cases:
Let g=x^(7q)+1, b'=1+x^2+x^3. The identity(x+1)b b'=x^7+1 shows b,b' divide g.
The binary row-rank defect of X=[A|b*I] is the binary dimension of ker X*.
Here X* is the vertical stack[A*;bI]. Thus a check-coordinate column alpha
satisfies b alpha=0 and A*alpha=0.
In F2[x]/g, every scalar annihilator element of b has a unique expression
 (g/b) beta mod g, beta in F2[x]/b.
This follows directly by polynomial cancellation b*a multiple of g iff a is
multiple of g/b, with beta unique modulo b; no coprimality is assumed.
Consequently the alpha conditions reduce to (A* mod b)*beta=0 over Q=F2[x]/b.
Because x^7=1 in Q and all A labels are0 or7, A* mod b=H^T, with H the binary
star incidence matrix. Its binary rank is7. Binary invertible row/column
operations reduce H^T to diag(I7,0) and stay invertible over Q. Its kernel is
Q^3, dimension3*deg(b)=9 over F2. Therefore rank X=10N-9.
For Z=[bI|A*], the adjoint Z* is the vertical stack[b*I;A]. Since
b*=x^-3 b', its scalar annihilator equals that of b'. Reduction modulo b'
gives A mod b'=H and again a9-dimensional binary check kernel. Thus
rank Z=10N-9. This uses the column-polynomial convention x^t for fiber t,
so adjoint means the ordinary binary transpose; exponent signs are explicit.
Thus k=20N-2(10N-9)=18 for every q>=2, even q included. The elementary polynomial
factorization and binary rank are exact finite ingredients; the quotient-ring
argument is an ordinary written proof, not a general Lean rank theorem.

Distance proof: the six base variables{2,3,5,6,7,8} form a binary dependency and
avoid both modified variables0,4. Put each at fiber0. Their six actual X columns
therefore sum to zero for every q. The resulting left-only word is outside the
Z row span: the left component of every binary Z row combination, equivalently every
column in im Z*, is divisible by b' in R_N (b*=x^-3 b'). Reduction modulo
b' sends it to zero, whereas our nonzero constant
coordinate vector survives. This quotient map is well-defined because b'|g.
It is a nontrivial weight-six logical. Odd kernel weights are excluded by
column3, weight2 by linearity, and weight4 by the proven elimination. Hence
minimum CSS distance is6. We obtain [[20N,18,6]]=[[140q,18,6]].
This is a constant-dimension, constant-distance family; its rate tends to zero.
It is not proposed as an asymptotically good family or a code-performance record.
Independent finite consumers q=2,3,4 confirm the formulas; exhaustive C++
quadruple/rank/logical verification is only claimed for q=2. Exact all-q rank,
distance and mixed-support arguments remain written, with the formal and finite
components separated in verification.json and the paper.
