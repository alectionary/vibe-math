\subsection{Dependency structure: from checks to decoder ambiguity}
The two headline theorems follow this chain, with no decoder-specific convergence premise:
\[
\begin{gathered}
 H_XH_Z^T=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad R_X\perp R_Z,\\
 R_X=R_Z\quad\Longrightarrow\quad w\in R_Z\Rightarrow w\cdot w=0
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad |w|\equiv0\pmod2,\\
 |a|\equiv1\pmod2,\ H_Xa=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 a\in R_X^\perp\setminus R_Z,\\
 H_Xa=H_X0,\ a\notin R_Z\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \text{no shared syndrome correction succeeds on both }0\text{ and }a.
\end{gathered}
\]
The source-to-formal path is separate: an actual check's induced degree is the weight of its intersection with the selected support, its parity is the binary dot product, and having no odd checks is exactly zero syndrome. These bridges prevent replacing ambient stabilizer membership by an induced submatrix condition. The strict-majority absorbing property is not needed after zero syndrome and oddness are known.
